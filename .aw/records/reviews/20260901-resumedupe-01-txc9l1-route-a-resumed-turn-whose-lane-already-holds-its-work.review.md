needed. Author-phase `aw ipd lint` returned clean and verified, and so did the `review-finalize`
checkpoint after revisions.

The plan's PURPOSE is sound and its central architectural instinct is right: move the resume decision
from the agent's judgment to the driver, which already holds the observable facts. F-1 and F-2 are
verified true, so the "just tell the agent" alternative is genuinely exhausted. The underlying bug is
also genuinely real, though not where the plan said (PR-002).

passed (PR-001). That is the finding that mattered, and it was found by MEASURING the allocation path
rather than reading the plan's reasoning, which was internally consistent and wrong.
