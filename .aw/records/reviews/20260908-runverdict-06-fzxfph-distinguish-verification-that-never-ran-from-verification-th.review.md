WHAT THIS ROUND CHANGED ABOUT THE PLAN'S SHAPE. Nothing about the design. The pattern in the revisions
is that this plan asked its executor to investigate several things the repository already answers, and
in one case the true answer reverses the instruction attached to it: the interrupt site is NOT protected
by the indeterminate machinery, so it belongs in the split, and the spec constraint bites on the LABEL
rather than on inclusion. The second theme is that a labelling plan must check where its labels are
CONSUMED: the record-not-the-gate justification holds only when validation is on, and a new token renders
as a bare dash in the one operator surface this plan exists to improve. The third is the Set-level
hazard: three plans now converge on one fifteen-line block, and coordinating with one of two siblings is
not enough to keep a condition from being named twice.
