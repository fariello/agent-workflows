## Round 1

All claims re-measured at HEAD `73e370ae` in an isolated lane worktree. The target plan was committed
and byte-identical to the driver's staged input, so the pre-review snapshot was correctly skipped per
Step 1. Structural preflight `aw ipd lint --phase author` reported `conforming` before review and
`--phase review-finalize` reported `conforming` after the revisions.

THE PLAN'S PURPOSE IS CORRECT AND IMPORTANT, and that is worth establishing first because most of what
follows is criticism of its numbers rather than of its existence. `runner_shared.evaluate_set_retirement`
run against this repo returns `eligible=False`, `reason=unfinished-children`, `detail="Set 'rununify' has
1 child(ren) that are not 'executed': 40it5e (to-review)"`. Eleven of twelve children are `executed`, so
this plan is LITERALLY the only thing holding the parent's retirement, and the parent's E-02 and E-03 will
tick unperformed the moment it finalizes unless it performs them. The plan understands its own mechanism
correctly.

THE DOMINANT FINDING IS THAT THE PLAN COMMITTED, IN ITS OWN HEADLINE, THE EXACT ERROR IT EXISTS TO CATCH.
Its Concern, Goal, F-02 and OQ-01's opening all led with "58 symbols still defined in BOTH runners, 2711
agy definition lines ... on that count it is NOT met" - and OQ-01's own resolution paragraph, three
sections later, already recorded that figure as wrong. A reader stopping at the Concern (which is what a
board view and the `aw attention` surface show) would carry away a verdict the plan itself had retracted.
I re-derived the measurement independently rather than adopting either number: of 58 symbols defined in
both runners (2757 agy definition lines), 34 have BOTH sides delegating into `runner_shared` and 1 has one
side delegating. That is the SANCTIONED de-duplicated form in this Set - the parent's own child-breakdown
note records the maintainer's 2026-09-03 `818uru` OQ-02 ruling as establishing "one real function in
`runner_shared`, one binding line per host" - so a name count reports the extraction's SUCCESS as its
failure. The genuine residue is 23 symbols / 661 agy lines, and under a strict test (neither side calls any
shared symbol AND host-token-normalized similarity >= 0.95) only two qualify, one of which is pinned
immovable. Every large function the headline leaned on delegates on both sides: `run_queue` makes 8
`runner_shared` calls on EACH side (OQ-01 says 10), and `main`, `build_parser`, `expand_selectors` and
`reclaim_lanes_on_interrupt` all delegate too.

A SECOND, INDEPENDENT FAILURE MADE E-03 UNEXECUTABLE AS WRITTEN, and it is the finding I would have raised
even if every number had been right. E-03 directed the executor to pin four symbols the parent measured as
uncovered on the agy side. Two of them no longer exist as forks: `extract_session_id` is defined ONLY in
`runner_shared` (absent from both runners), and `build_prompt`'s agy definition is a single delegating
statement. `integrate_lane_branch` is a 25-line wrapper whose own docstring says "the IMPLEMENTATION is the
single shared `runner_shared.integrate_lane_branch`". A characterization suite over those would exercise
`runner_shared` through two aliases while reporting two-host coverage - a test that manufactures confidence,
which is strictly worse than the absence it replaces, and is the same class of unfalsifiable evidence the
parent's own completion criteria warn about ("the `2c122z` plan-review found a `grep "return True"` proof
that could never fail").

THE CITED PRECEDENT WAS DELETED ON PURPOSE FOUR DAYS BEFORE THE PLAN WAS AUTHORED. `tests/test_wtiso_characterization.py`
is gone, removed in `d4dd6b88` (2026-09-18) "retire change-detector tests over code text and prose", whose
message records that it "asserted three diagnosed DEFECTS still exist" for fix-plans that are all
superseded. The same sweep, and `7ebc2964` before it, folded four `test_rununify_*_characterization.py`
files into their behavioral twins while deleting `len(getsourcelines(main)) == 309`-style pins,
`SequenceMatcher` ratio assertions and frozen AST closure censuses. An executor following the citation
would re-create the retired form under a new filename, reverting a maintainer decision. Note this does NOT
contradict the parent's "source-reading tests are work, not vetoes" ruling: that governs RE-BASING an
existing pin, while `d4dd6b88` governs AUTHORING a new one. Both are now stated in the plan's conventions.

Verified TRUE and left standing: the plan's reading of the orchestrator coverage gate and the retirement
mechanism; F-05's characterization of the parent's declared E-01 gap; OQ-01's resolution and its recorded
maintainer quote; the existence and adequacy of `runresidue` (`gqo6if`) as the residue carrier, which does
name `reclaim_lanes_on_interrupt` first and does report its own residue as a 7-to-20 range with both tests
stated; and the plan's refusal to unify anything itself, which is the right call and is why its measurement
can be trusted.
