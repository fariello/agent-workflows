message requirement is correct and is the kind of detail that decides whether a guard survives.

WHAT THIS ROUND CHANGED ABOUT THE PLAN'S SHAPE. Nothing about the design, everything about the
measurement and the guard's mechanics. The pattern across PR-C01, PR-C02 and PR-C03 is one thing:
every naive way of counting this coupling under-reports it, and each under-count would have produced
a guard that passes while freezing a stale surface. The count was short by one; a body-only AST walk
is short by five; a bound-name key collapses four into one. So the revisions are systematic rather
than arithmetic: the set is now defined as whatever `ast.walk` reports over `alias.name` at execution
time, the mutation check gained the one mutation that distinguishes a correct guard from a plausible
wrong one, and every count in the plan is framed as a snapshot to re-derive. That the plan's own
missing name is the same one whose earlier omission from a guard caused a real defect is the sharpest
possible illustration of why the freeze guard, and not the classification, is this child's deliverable.
