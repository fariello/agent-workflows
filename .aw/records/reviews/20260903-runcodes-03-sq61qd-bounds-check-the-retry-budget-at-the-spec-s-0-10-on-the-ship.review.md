## Round 1

All claims verified at HEAD `42f728817f8f13acb8e7a314eaa3383fb7edbcab`, working tree clean, target plan
committed and unchanged, so the pre-review snapshot was correctly skipped per Step 1. Structural
preflight `aw ipd lint --phase author --agent` reported `conforming` before review and
`--phase review-finalize` reported `conforming` after the revisions.

This is the strongest-measured of the three `runcodes` children, and it deserves saying: every line
citation in it was EXACT, which is rare in this Set's history of inherited counts. `DEFAULT_RETRY_LIMIT`
is `2` at `:62`, `plan_retry` is at `:213` with `limit` at `:219`, `retry_budget_remaining` is at
`:355-356`, and neither validates. I also ran F-1's two behavioral claims rather than reading them:
`plan_retry(limit=-1)` really does raise `RetryLimitExceededError` reporting "0 retries recorded
meets/exceeds limit -1" on the first retry, and `limit=10_000` really is accepted by both helpers,
returning a remaining budget of 10000. The plan's characterization of both hazards is accurate.

One finding changed the design, and it came from reading the plan against its declared CONSUMER rather
than against the module. PR-001: the plan says to add the check to the two helper bodies, but
`runflags-01` (`uyeko5`) E-04 is written to CALL this validation and its own fence forbids a duplicate
bound - and `--retry-budget` validates an operator-supplied value at parse time, when no `RunEngine`
or `step_id` exists. A check inside `plan_retry(engine, step_id, ...)` is therefore unreachable from
the flag layer, and that plan would have been forced into exactly the duplication it prohibits. The
fix is small (name the validator) but it is a real interface decision, not a wording change.

Three findings are of the "the test would pass against the bug" class this plan is otherwise good at
avoiding: a reusable-error conflation (PR-002), the `bool`-is-an-`int` hole (PR-003), and an
accepted-boundary case with no behavioral assertion (PR-004).

I also resolved the plan's one open question from repository evidence rather than asking, because
another plan already owns the work.
