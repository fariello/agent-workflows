- `tests/test_suite_baseline.py` was deleted by `19313eed7`. `tests/test_agent_surface_conformance.py::test_agent_surface_conformance_mutation` runs every `mutation` leaf with bare `--agent`; `integration-lock` carries an `arg_fixture_needed` exemption in `tests/conformance_matrix.py`.
