`aw ipd lint --phase author --agent` reported `conforming` (exit 0, zero findings) BEFORE semantic
review, and `--phase review-finalize` reports `conforming` after revision. This plan's own first

EXACTLY. I re-derived every load-bearing measurement with my own probes rather than trusting the
findings table, and the plan's willingness to contradict its own backlog item turns out to be its
best feature:

WHERE I WENT BEYOND THE PLAN, the two HIGH findings both came from asking a question the plan never
asked: what ELSE is approved and about to touch these files. `aw find plans 76ic0k` (reached from the
precedent test's own docstring, which names it) turned up an approved sibling that declares
`CONTRIBUTING.md`, appends to the same bullet list, ships an AST guard over `tests/test_*.py` with a
one-entry allowlist, and says "do NOT edit `GUIDING_PRINCIPLES.md`". That is PR-101 and PR-102.

I also ran two probes the plan asserted but had not demonstrated, and both confirmed it: the
in-loop bare-`assert` hazard (`ROWS_EXECUTED=['row a: passes', 'row b: fails']`, so row c never ran)
and the unittest half, where I found the reported total is invocation-dependent.

THE PATTERN WORTH NAMING, because it is what most of the MEDIUM findings share: FOUR of this plan's
own censuses are wrong or unreproducible (F-02's 165, F-07's `99 of 131` and its "four loops", F-08's
file membership, F-09's single deletion), and every one is a LIVE POPULATION that drifted between
authoring and review. The plan is otherwise scrupulous about citing by symbol and quoted content, so
the lesson is not carelessness but genre: a count is the one kind of claim that rots silently. The
durable fix is not better counting, it is the gate rule I added forbidding any census number in the
COMMITTED PROSE, since `GUIDING_PRINCIPLES.md` is the worst possible home for a figure nobody will
re-measure. The findings table keeps the numbers, where they are dated and checkable.

ONE THING I DELIBERATELY DID NOT FLAG. The plan ships no mechanical enforcement that a table obeys
the documented shape, and that is correct rather than under-scope: the arity is variable (7 of 16
files carry `needles`/`forbidden`), the first column takes 22 measured spellings, and the nearest
in-tree precedent for a mechanical test-shape refusal (`76ic0k`) spends three E-items bounding its
own detector and ships an allowlist to avoid condemning conforming code. I strengthened the
Deferred row's reasoning with those measurements rather than treating the absence as a gap.
