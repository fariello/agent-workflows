## Round 1

Reviewed at HEAD `8108fb87`; the plan was committed and unchanged, so no pre-review snapshot was
needed. Author-phase `aw ipd lint` returned clean and verified, and so did the `review-finalize`
checkpoint after revisions.

The plan is unusually well-researched and several of its harder claims hold up under measurement: F-1
(`ITEM_DEP_TYPES` really is `("ipd", "spec", "backlog")`), F-2 (spec 5.4 rule 4 and the deliberate
type-rank omission in `queue_sort_key`), F-4 (all four `xprio` plans really do pin the sort key as
unchanged, three with observed evidence), F-6, and the `slow`-marker warning about the undeclared-leaf
guards are all verified true. Its instinct to make every order an explicit opt-in with the current
sort as default is the right call and preserves a live contract by construction.

Two things were nevertheless wrong in ways that mattered. The headline key, `-o depth`, could not be
computed from the data the plan said it would use, and the natural workaround silently breaks the
single-authority rule the same plan spends a paragraph defending (PR-001). And `todo`, which the plan
treats as an existing alias to redeclare, is actually a divergent parser whose promised
byte-identical behavior is measurably false today (PR-002). Both were found by running the code, not
by reading the prose.
