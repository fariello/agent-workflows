Reviewed in an isolated review lane at HEAD `7d035a755`. The plan file was committed and the tree

THE PLAN'S CENTRAL JUDGEMENT IS CORRECT, AND THE EVIDENCE FOR ITS HARDEST CALL IS BETTER THAN THE
PLAN CLAIMS. The backlog item offered a binary ("delete both lines or re-justify them") and the plan
returned a split answer on measurement. I re-derived the mechanism that makes the split legitimate
and it reproduces exactly:

- `tools/ipdrunner/runagy.py` copies the whole module namespace, private names included
  (`for _k, _v in vars(agy_runipd).items(): if not _k.startswith("__"): globals()[_k] = _v`).
- `tools/ipdrunner/runipd.py` binds FIVE named attributes and nothing else (`main`, `DriverError`,
  `Palette`, `Heartbeat`, `PlanRecord`).

So an underscore prefix means "unreachable" for `oc_runipd` and means nothing at all for
`agy_runipd`. That asymmetry is the whole decision, the plan identified it as "the single most
decision-relevant convention found", and it is right.

Everything else material also held on independent re-derivation:

- F-2: `test_runagy.py:268` reads `driver._read_id(text)` and asserts `"a1b2c3"`, with
  `import runagy as driver` at line 15.
- F-3: running that one node gives `AttributeError: module 'runagy' has no attribute '_read_status'`
  at line 271, i.e. `_read_id` PASSES and the failure is one assertion later, exactly as recorded.
- F-4: the whole `hasattr` matrix reproduces, including the non-obvious half (`_read_status` and
  `_read_deps` absent from `oc_runipd`, `agy_runipd` AND `runner_shared`).
- F-6: `len(oc_runipd.__all__)` is 22 with no `_read*` entry, neither host is in
  `agent_workflows.__all__`, and `agy_runipd` declares no `__all__`.
- Step 0: both malformed `# noqa` warnings are present at the two cited lines, and
  `testpaths = ["tests"]` is at `pyproject.toml:154`.
- The maintainer ruling is in the item's own history verbatim: "We do not test to make sure code
  does not change or pin imports."

I ALSO PROVED THE RETENTION LOAD-BEARING IN A WAY THE PLAN DID NOT, because V-03(c) as authored only
showed the name resolves today. Deleting it in memory (`del agy_runipd._read_id`, then importing the
shim) makes `driver._read_id` raise `AttributeError`, which is the positive proof that the re-export
is consumed rather than merely present. That is now V-03(e).

THE HIGHEST FINDING IS A VALIDATION TRAP RATHER THAN A DESIGN FLAW. V-02(d) told the executor to
match the authored literal `3387 passed, 2 skipped` and to treat any change as a failure that "must
be investigated, not explained away". The tree now measures `3863 passed`, 667 commits later, so a
correct no-op change would have presented as a 476-test regression with the V-item's own wording
forbidding the executor from dismissing it. This is the recurring class in this repository's plans
(a live count frozen into an acceptance bar), and the durable fix is self-relative comparison, which
I applied to every count in the plan.

ONE METHOD CORRECTION WORTH NAMING SEPARATELY. F-5 established its best finding by deleting both
import lines ON DISK and running the suite. The finding is true, but the method is the one
AGENTS.md's shared-checkout rule exists to prevent on two of the highest-contention files in the
repo, and `t0ovw6`'s review already established the in-memory form for this exact case. I re-proved
the claim with a pytest plugin (plugin arm `11 failed, 3852 passed, 2 skipped`; control arm with no
plugin, identical), which both confirms F-5 and demonstrates the method the executor must use. The
11 failures are artifacts of invoking pytest from a scratch subdirectory and appear in BOTH arms;
the clean bare run is green.

WHAT I DELIBERATELY DID NOT FLAG. The plan declines to author any test defending the retained
re-export, which leaves it protected by a comment and by a caller that does not pass. That is not an
under-scope gap: the maintainer's ruling forbids the pinning test, and P16 forbids it independently.
I recorded the residual honestly in the scope check (nothing mechanical stops a future `ruff --fix`
once the `# noqa` goes) rather than treating it as a defect to fix, because the alternative is the
test the maintainer ruled out.
