### Notes

THE PLAN'S CORE JUDGEMENT SURVIVED REVIEW, and it is worth stating plainly because the two blockers are
loud. Its central design choice, to COMPUTE the readiness from conditions and cap the write at
`go-pending-approval` rather than grant an agent a licence to edit the field, is correct and is the
only shape that does not reintroduce the forgery the repository forbids. Its refusal to re-review the
nine (on measured evidence that the reviews swept complete finding sets) is also correct, and PR-001 does
not disturb it: the finding is that the plan overstated the OUTCOME, not that the remedy is wrong.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE PLAN'S PURPOSE. Before review it read as a verb that unsticks nine plans. After
review it is a verb that removes the STALE component of a refusal so that any REMAINING cause becomes
visible and attributable, which for eight of the nine means the plan stays `no-go` for a reason a human
can now see and act on. That is a smaller claim and a truer one, and it is what E-06's per-plan reason
table now delivers.

FOUR OF THE NINE STRANDED PLANS PREDATE THIS SESSION (`8tgg6g`, `dw7i3m`, `daexj1`, `u06zo2`), which is
independent corroboration that the accumulation is structural rather than an artifact of one answering
