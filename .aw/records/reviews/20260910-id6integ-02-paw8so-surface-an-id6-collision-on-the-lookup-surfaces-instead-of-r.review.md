## Round 1

Reviewed at HEAD `a5a5f26b`. The target plan was committed and unchanged, so the pre-review snapshot was
correctly skipped. `aw ipd lint` conformed at `--phase author` before review and again at
`--phase review-finalize` after revisions. `aw sanitize --agent` clean.

THE PLAN'S CENTRAL THESIS HELD, and that is worth recording because it is the reason the plan exists.
`aw find uyeko5` still prints THREE rows at exit 0 from three genuinely different causes; the review
convention really is documented (`reviews/README.md`) and really would be mass-flagged by the backlog
item's proposed message; both of the item's original examples really are gone (`y6mfgo` and `ntf6sx`
each return one plan today); `aw attention` reports `valid: True`. The plan's decision to refuse the
item's one-size warning and turn the work on DISCRIMINATION rather than detection is correct.

THE DISCRIMINATOR IT CHOSE, HOWEVER, WAS FALSIFIED (PR-001), and that is the finding that reshaped the
plan. `kind=substring` does not mean "is a reference"; it means "not proven to declare". 63 of the 608
plans that genuinely declare a bullet `- Id:` resolve as `substring` purely because the declaration
sits past the 4096-byte bounded read, and 52 of them produce a multi-row `find`. A new E-06 supplies a
reliable per-row declaration predicate that E-01 now depends on.

Two findings would have shipped user-visible wrong behavior: a remedy command that is a measured no-op
on the shape it would be printed for (PR-002), and a promised machine signal on a surface that cannot
carry one (PR-004). One finding corrected an overstated reuse claim that would have misled the executor
about what `resolve_for_mutation` actually detects (PR-003).
