## Round 1

The plan's premise is correct and its five findings all reproduce at lane HEAD `2f0b4e9f`: the
registry yields 9 types with a `group` verb over 3 distinct backends, all three carry the setid-length
guard, `comms` is correctly excluded, `tests/test_artifact_group.py` was deleted in `19313eed`, no test
drives `aw group`, and every one of the 9 types was re-measured refusing a 26-character setid and
warning at 16. The deliverable is right-sized and the scope (one test file, no production change) is
honest.

Three findings are substantive. The WARN assertion as written could not distinguish a warning from a
refusal; only the firing side of each threshold was tested, which a guard that fired on every setid
would satisfy; and E-03's isolation mechanism mutated state shared with every other agent in this
checkout.
