## Round 1

Structural preflight `aw ipd lint --phase author` reported `conforming` before review and

THE PLAN SHOULD EXIST AND ITS PREMISE IS SOUND, established first because most of what follows is
criticism of its mechanisms rather than of its purpose. The parent `s0gnha` genuinely carries two items no
child owned, and the sharper of the two is a release-gate-bearing record change. Every structural claim
verified exactly: `runner_shared.evaluate_set_retirement(repo, 'reaskscore')` returns `eligible=False`,
`reason=unfinished-children`; `yxfw4k` is `graduated` and `x7wfyx` is `open`, both carrying
`Blocks-Release: next` and both `Work-Kind: bug`; the parent's E-02 and E-03 are lifted faithfully rather
than reinvented, and its E-01 is correctly excluded as dispatch ordering an agent cannot perform. The
`x7wfyx` item A / item B split the plan relies on is in that item's own "What is still missing" section,
word for word.

THE DOMINANT FINDING IS THAT THE PLAN'S SAFETY ARGUMENT RESTS ON A TOOL REFUSAL THAT DOES NOT ARRIVE. E-02
asserted that `aw backlog set done yxfw4k` "FAILS CLOSED unless the gate is provably preserved or released
by one of the three documented fixes". That is true of the rule in general and FALSE of this item. Calling
the shipped predicate directly, `check_engine.evaluate_blocking_close(repo, yxfw4k, "done", evidence=None)`
returns legitimate via route HANDOFF with reason "gate 'next' handed off to a From-Backlog plan or spec" -
no evidence argument, no flag, no warning. The cause is structural: `find_from_backlog_artifacts` finds FOUR
plans carrying `From-Backlog: yxfw4k` with the same `Blocks-Release: next` (`skn8uk`, `ty7w6o`, `s0gnha`,
`svacmz`), and the predicate checks HANDOFF before SATISFIED, so the handoff route short-circuits. Two
aggravating measurements: two of those four carriers (`s0gnha`, `svacmz`) are themselves still `approved` in
`pending/`, so the gate is being preserved by plans that have not run; and the plan's own E-02 expected
outcome asserted the gate "was satisfied by citation", which is a route the predicate will not report here.
The consequence is concrete rather than theoretical. This plan's OQ-01 correctly answers that an absent
`svacmz` evidence block must NOT close `yxfw4k` - and nothing but the executor enforces that answer, on an
item that is a release-blocking `bug`.

THE SECOND FINDING IS THAT THE RECEIPT CHECK WAS WEAKER THAN THE BAR IT CHECKS, in the precise place
`svacmz`'s own review closed a hole. E-01 asked for "the five shared constants' ACTUAL VALUES". `svacmz`'s
current `V-01` demands EIGHT definition sites across three files and states in terms that "a paste covering
only five sites" FAILS, because two of the five names are multiply defined and the omitted third
`TERMINAL_STATES` copy is the one `reconcile_disposition` reads - the exact one-sided-widening hole its
2026-09-19 review found. A receipt check that accepted five sites would launder that hole back open.
Symmetrically, E-01 demanded "the `git diff` for both pin files" while `svacmz`'s `V-02` was deliberately
re-expressed at ASSERTION level after its review measured the empty-diff bar already unsatisfiable (3
insertions / 2 deletions from unrelated main commit `eee6f427`, zero assertion lines), so this plan would
have manufactured a failure its owner explicitly ruled out. Both directions come from the same root cause:
the receipt check paraphrased a bar instead of quoting it.

A THIRD, NON-DEFECT FINDING WORTH RECORDING because it changes what the executor should expect. The plan is
not runnable at review HEAD: `oc_runipd.dependency_status_detailed` reports `executed:svacmz` unsatisfied
("external target svacmz is 'approved' (directory 'pending'), needs one of ['executed']"), and `dy9ymn` is
outstanding too, so TWO siblings precede it rather than one. That is the runner's business and not a plan
defect - dependency depth is its first sort key and edges are re-checked at dispatch - but it means E-01 is a
receipt check over evidence that does not exist yet: all four of `svacmz`'s `Observed evidence` blocks are
empty with all four `Result: pending`. So executed today, this plan can only take its failure path, which is
correct behavior and is now stated so nobody mistakes it for a malfunction.

Verified TRUE and left standing: the OQ-01 answer and its reasoning; the deferral of the parent's E-01; the
refusal to re-perform `svacmz`'s verifications; the `graduated`-not-`done` decision for `x7wfyx`; and all
four declared `Scope-Paths`, each of which is genuinely needed because `aw backlog set` MOVES an item between
directories.
