## Round 1

All claims re-derived at HEAD `d39d58e9`. The plan cites `cfc7f5c1`, which is a real commit one day

so several of the plan's numbers were stale before review even began. The target plan was committed and
unchanged, so the pre-review snapshot was correctly skipped per Step 1. Structural preflight

THE CENTRAL DIAGNOSIS IS CORRECT AND I VERIFIED IT INDEPENDENTLY RATHER THAN TRUSTING THE TABLE.
`ipd_lifecycle._plan_status_events` still ends with the comment "Inline history is stored newest-first;
reverse to oldest-first for derivation." and `events.reverse()`, and the probe still inverts: a
two-line oldest-first history returns `['to-review', 'draft']`. The four-writer disagreement the
backlog item `tk1gqo` documents is real, the per-block direction algorithm is a sound response to it,
the unordered-tie rule is what approved spec `2vev8j` 4.7 actually requires, and both alternatives the
plan rejects (a rank-free date sort, a lifecycle-rank tiebreak) are correctly rejected for the reasons
given. The author's reasoning about the DEFECT is good. What review found is that the PROPOSED FIX, as
written, breaks something else and that four of its five fixtures and its abstention mechanism would
not have worked as specified.

THE DOMINANT FINDING IS PR-601, and it is the one a re-reviewer should check first. E-02 said to "keep
`_plan_status_events` as the flattened oldest-first list of those groups, so `derive_plan_status` keeps
its signature." The signature is not the issue; the ANSWER is. `_plan_status_events` has two consumers
with incompatible needs: `check_lifecycle_transitions`, which wants an order it can trust and must
ABSTAIN when it has none, and `derive_plan_status`, which wants one current status and has no abstain
state. I implemented the plan's own algorithm exactly as specified and measured the flattened result
against `derive_plan_status` over all 776 plans: 307 answers change, 128 of them to an EARLIER
lifecycle status. Eight of today's 49 pending plans regress, and they regress on the SHIPPED SHAPE `aw
set` writes - two records, same date, newest-first (`- 2026-09-25 reviewed (...)` above `- 2026-09-25
to-review (...)`). That group is single-date and multi-status, so the plan's own rule makes it
`ordered=False` with members in file order, and the flattened last-on-sequence status becomes
`to-review` against an authoritative `- Status: reviewed`. Today all 49 derive correctly. So a plan
whose stated purpose is removing false consistency findings would have manufactured eight new ones in
the very lane its rule scopes to, and the fix for that is not a better flattening: I measured all four
candidate derivation rules against the authoritative field over 743 plans and the SHIPPED reversal is
the joint best (229 mismatches; plain file order 507, first-record 229, date sort 245). The reversal is
wrong as an ordering rule and right as the derivation rule, which is why the plan now keeps it and
guards it.

THE GUARD NEEDED TO BE A BASELINE, NOT AN ASSERTION, because nothing in `tests/` covers any of these
symbols. A grep of the whole test tree for `derive_plan_status`, `derive_status_from_events`,
`_plan_status_events`, `check_lifecycle_transitions` and `lifecycle-transition-invalid` returns ZERO
hits. So there was no existing test to lean on and no way to notice a 307-plan behavior change; the
suite passes `1950 passed, 1 skipped` either way. E-01 now captures a whole-tree derivation baseline as
a tracked fixture at the PRE-CHANGE head and E-02 asserts equality against it, with V-01 requiring
`git status` evidence that both production files were unmodified at capture time. That provenance
evidence is load-bearing: a baseline captured after the reader changed records the new behavior as if
it were the old, turning the only guard into a tautology.

PR-603 IS THE SECOND MATERIAL FINDING AND IT IS A FALSIFIABILITY DEFECT. Fixture (e), the plan's proof
that a same-date tie is treated as unordered, was specified as `to-review` above `draft`, both
`2026-09-01`. Measured on the CURRENT reader, that shape yields ZERO findings: the reversal flips it to
`['draft', 'to-review']`, which is a legal forward edge. So the test the plan describes as a negative
control passes before and after the change and proves nothing, while E-01's expected outcome claims it
"fails with a finding." The shape that actually discriminates is `draft` above `approved`, same date,
which yields one `approved -> draft` finding today and zero under the proposal. I measured both.

PR-604 IS AN ABSTENTION MECHANISM THAT DOES THE OPPOSITE OF ABSTAINING. E-03 said an unordered group
should "reset `prev` to the group's single status when it has one distinct forward status, else to
`None`, meaning the next edge is unvalidated." But `None` already MEANS something to
`validate_transition`: it is the first-recorded-event case, and it REJECTS every target except `draft`
with "missing predecessor: cannot start the lifecycle at ...". I prototyped both readings over the
whole tree: an explicit suppression flag gives 296 flagged edges, the literal `prev=None` reading gives
511. The plan's abstain path, implemented as written, manufactures 215 findings. This is the finding
most likely to survive into a working-looking implementation, because every other assertion in the new
test file still passes, which is why V-06 now requires a dedicated abstention probe.

PR-602 IS A COMPLETENESS GAP IN THE BACKWARD-EDGE TABLE, and it matters because the table is the
mechanism spec `2vev8j` 4.8 point 3 chose INSTEAD of rank ordering, so an omission is not noise, it is
an illegal edge. `_LEGAL_BACKWARD_EDGES` was specified as `{("approved", "reviewed")}` alone.
`auto-approved` shares rank 3 with `approved` (`_status_rank` returns 3 for both), spec `25kzda` 4.5's
`IPD-AUTO-APPROVAL` check prescribes recovery with `aw ipd set reviewed <id6> --message "invalid
automated approval"`, and `validate_transition("auto-approved","reviewed")` is refused today. So the
plan would have shipped a fail-closed table that leaves the automated tier's own documented recovery
procedure illegal. This repository has recorded the identical mistake once already: the apprvguard gate
comment in `status_set` states that "a gate keyed on the literal `approved` would leave the automated
tier ungated," which is why that gate keys on `ipd_schema.READY_TO_EXECUTE` and never on one spelling.

PR-605 AND PR-606 WOULD EACH HAVE COST THE EXECUTOR A DEBUGGING DETOUR ON A FALSE TRAIL. First,
`validate_transition` refuses a terminal transition by any actor outside `_FINALIZE_ACTORS`, and it
does so BEFORE the predecessor logic, so a fixture writing `2026-09-04 executed (opencode/model)`
fails with `unauthorized terminal transition` and not with an ordering verdict; the plan's fixtures
named no actors. Second, the plan's block rule ("a blank or non-record line ends a block") fragments
real files: 120 plans in the tree carry 1097 non-blank non-record lines inside `## Workflow history`,
which are wrapped continuations of long records, and each one would split a logical block into
fragments whose direction is then "unknown". ZERO pending plans carry them, so the defect is invisible
in exactly the lane the rule scopes to and would surface only in the whole-tree measurement E-08
performs.

PR-607 IS A MEASUREMENT THAT WAS TAKEN THE WRONG WAY AND IS QUOTED THROUGHOUT THE PLAN. The plan's
whole-tree figures (F-4's 435 edges, F-6's "22 flagged plans") were measured calling
`validate_transition(prev, status)` WITHOUT `actor=`. The production checker passes `actor=actor`. With
the real call the current predicate flags 846 edges on 617 plans, not 435 on 270, and the proposed
design leaves 296 edges on 291 plans, not 22 on 22. Worse, the composition is misleading in the
author's favor: of those 296 survivors, 286 are `unauthorized terminal` actor rejections that this plan
does not address and only 10 are `backwards`. An execution report pasting "22" or even "296" as a bare
total could not distinguish a working ordering fix from a broken one, so V-08 now requires the
breakdown by rejection reason and an explicit statement of whether `actor=` was passed.

PR-608 IS AN HONESTY FINDING RATHER THAN A CORRECTNESS ONE, and I raise it because the plan's headline
("pending 0") invites a reading the evidence does not support. Abstaining on unknowable order reduces
what is checked: I counted the edges the checker VALIDATES AT ALL and it drops from 30 to 22 in
`pending/` and from 1946 to 722 tree-wide, a 63% reduction. That is the correct treatment per spec
`2vev8j` 4.7 and it is also a coverage loss whose recovery is the `seq` journal deferred to `ms06pi`.
The plan now says so, and the gate's approval paragraph puts it in front of the human.
