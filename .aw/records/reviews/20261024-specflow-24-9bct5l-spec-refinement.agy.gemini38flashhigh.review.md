### Scope and Verification Summary

1. **Two-Pass Hierarchical Synthesis Mathematical Turn Bounds Formula (F-121)**: In Section 4.3.1, the mathematical formula for `Max_Turns` assumed monolithic, single-pass synthesis (`[ (1 if N_participants > 1 else 0) * (1 + Max_Retries) ]`). However, FR-34 mandates a two-pass hierarchical synthesis when context volume exceeds 80% of budget, which dispatches 1 matrix compilation turn followed by up to M section drafting turns (M <= 3, yielding up to 4 turns before retries). Section 4.3.1 noted this in prose on line 282, but failed to incorporate `M_synth_sections` into the formal formula. Generalized Stage 3 in the `Max_Turns` formula to `[ ( (1 + M_synth_sections) if N_participants > 1 else 0) * (1 + Max_Retries) ]` (where `M_synth_sections = 0` for monolithic synthesis, and `1 <= M_synth_sections <= 3` for hierarchical synthesis), updated Worked Example 3 worst-case calculations (up to 87 turns before the global budget cap of 40), and verified that no runaway loop can bypass the budget ceiling.
2. **Flowchart 7.1 and 7.2 Terminal Graph Sinks and Research Transition Explicit Placement (F-122)**: In Flowcharts 7.1 and 7.2, cap-with-findings decision diamonds branched to halted states (`CapHalt_solo`, `CapHalt1`, `CapHalt2`, `CapHalt3`, `SynthCapHalt` in 7.1; `RC_solo_halt`, `RC_multi_halt`, `RM_halt` in 7.2), but these nodes had no outgoing edges to an End node, leaving dangling sinks in the flowchart graph. Connected these halted nodes to explicit terminal nodes (`Q_halt[End: Exit 1 in state capped_with_findings]` in Flowchart 7.1 and `Stop_halt[End: Exit 1 in state capped_with_findings]` in Flowchart 7.2). Furthermore, in Flowchart 7.2, the step moving finalized research reports from the flat hot root `.aw/records/research/` to the weekly shard directory `reference/<YYYYMM-Www>/` prior to indexing was omitted between contract validation (`RK_solo`, `RK`) and manifest indexing (`RL_solo`, `RL`), appearing only in terminal node labels. Added explicit transition nodes (`RMove_solo[Move to reference/YYYYMM-Www/]` and `RMove_multi[Move to reference/YYYYMM-Www/]`).
3. **State Diagram 7.3 Cap Transition Disambiguation against Blocker/High Halts (F-123)**: In State Diagram 7.3, transitions from `CappedCandidate` and `CappedSynthesis` to `Synthesizing` and `Finalizing` were labeled `Proceed with Warnings (N > 1)` and `Proceed with Prominent Warnings`. This created an apparent contradiction with FR-11 (Honest Cap Enforcement), which strictly forbids proceeding to synthesis or finalization when caps are reached with unresolved blocker or high findings. Clarified the transition labels to explicitly specify that proceeding to synthesis or finalization from capped states occurs ONLY when findings are advisory-only (`low`/`medium`) or when forced via `--allow-unresolved-findings`.
4. **CLI Table 3.4 Argument Symmetry and Defaults Completeness (F-124)**: Table 3.4 contained several option asymmetries: (a) in `aw specs flow prepare` and `aw research flow prepare`, `--slug <slug>` was marked without `(optional)` and `--set <setid>` without `(optional, default: specflow)`, whereas `new` derives slug if omitted and defaults set to `specflow`; (b) in `aw specs flow from-spec`, `--objective <str>` was listed without `(optional)`, contradicting FR-35 line 371; (c) in `aw specs flow merge` and `aw research flow reconcile`, `--synth-refine-cap` and `--reconcile-refine-cap` lacked default indicators (`default: 2`); (d) in `aw specs flow finalize` and `aw research flow finalize`, `--title <str> (optional)` and `--slug <slug> (optional)` were missing, preventing operators from supplying or refining title/slug metadata at finalization time; (e) in `aw specs flow new/prepare/from-spec` and `aw research flow new/prepare`, `--roster <roster-spec>` lacked explicit default resolution documentation (`default: preferred-three in thorough, quick-solo in quick`). Harmonized Table 3.4 arguments across all command rows.
5. **Research Artifact Matrix Single-Participant and Decomposed Mode Alignment (F-125)**: In Table 6.2 (Artifact Matrix): (a) candidate research report omitted `[.<ace>]` (`YYYYMMDD-<setid>-10-<id6>-cand.<model>.research-report.md`) while revised candidate included `[.<ace>]`. Harmonized candidate research report to `YYYYMMDD-<setid>-10-<id6>-cand[.<ace>].<model>.research-report.md`; (b) final research row only listed the multi-participant `reconciliation-report` row, omitting the single-participant final research report (`research-report`) mandated by FR-18B and tested in AT-43. Added the single-participant final research row `YYYYMMDD-<setid>-01-<id6>-<slug>[.<ace>].<model>.research-report.md`; (c) decomposed research candidate reports (`--investigation-mode decomposed`) were not explicitly represented in Table 6.2. Added `2. Candidate (Research Decomposed)` with pattern `YYYYMMDD-<setid>-10-<id6>-cand-<subtopic>[.<ace>].<model>.research-report.md`.
6. **Research Report Front Matter Status Lifecycle in Weekly Reference Shards (F-126)**: Section 6.3 listed front matter schemas for candidate and reconciled research reports with `status: active`. However, upon finalization and relocation to `reference/<YYYYMM-Www>/`, the research report's `status` field must transition from `active` to `reference` to comply with `research_contract.SHARDED_STATUSES` and avoid `check.lifecycle-placement-conflict`. Added the finalized research report front matter schema documenting `status: reference` and `outcome: informational`.
7. **TOML Model Normalization Key Coverage for Gemini 3.8 Flash (F-127)**: Section 11 Phase 1 listed normalizations for `gpt-6.1` and `claude-opus-5.5`, but omitted normalizations containing dots for the Gemini Flash High slot (`"gemini-3.8-flash" = "gemini38flash"`, `"gemini-3.8-flash-high" = "gemini38flashhigh"`). Because `runner-profiles.json` and Section 5.2 configure model `google/gemini-3.8-flash` with variant `high`, registering these dotted keys as quoted TOML strings in `research-models.toml` ensures that `normalize_model()` and filename token resolution succeed without warnings.
8. **Acceptance Test Matrix Scope Expansion (AT-57..AT-60) (F-128)**: Acceptance test matrix lacked explicit coverage for hierarchical synthesis mathematical turn bounds (`AT-57`), finalized research report front matter `status: reference` in weekly reference shards (`AT-58`), CLI prepare and finalize argument symmetry (`AT-59`), and Gemini 3.8 Flash dotted model TOML normalizations (`AT-60`). Added `AT-57` through `AT-60` to Section 9, updated Section 6.3 review front matter schema scope to `AT-01..AT-60`, updated Section 12.1 inspection baseline to `e12f81ac1f251eb3fa774e50bb2be7ee9ea8df42`, and updated Section 12.2 self-check item.
