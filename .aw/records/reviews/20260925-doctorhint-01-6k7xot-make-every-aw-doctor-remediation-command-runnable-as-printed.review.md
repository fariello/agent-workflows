## Round 1

The target plan was committed and unchanged, so the pre-review snapshot was correctly skipped per
Step 1. Structural preflight `aw ipd lint --phase author --agent` reported `conforming` (exit 0)
before review and again at `--phase review-finalize` after the revisions. The plan is `Kind: child`,
so the `IPD-S407` orchestrator checklist check does not apply.

THE PLAN'S DIAGNOSIS IS CORRECT AND ITS THREE MEASURED CLAIMS ALL REPRODUCE. `aw rename backlog <f>`
exits 2 with `error: at least one of --slug, --set, or --order is required to rename`
(`artifact_rename.compute_target_name`); `aw group backlog <f> --set newset` previews only and leaves
the file unchanged; `aw backlog set <f> --blocks-release next` exits 2 with `aw set: at least one
target selector (id6, setid, or filename) is required` (`status_set.py:1628`), and the same holds for
`specs`. The remedy it chooses (`command=None` plus a corrected shape in the fix text) matches the
in-function `id6-identity-slot` precedent and the `resolve_next_actions` contract that skips a falsy
command. So the approach is sound and was repaired rather than replanned.

WHAT REVIEW FOUND IS THAT THE PLAN UNDERSTATES BOTH THE DEFECT SET AND THE CORRECTNESS OF ITS OWN
PRESCRIBED SHAPES. Four of its five prescriptions were measured to be wrong or incomplete, and two
further branches carry the same class of defect and were unaddressed. The most serious, F-4, is a
remediation that exits 0 and reports success while leaving the finding in place, which is the exact
false-completion path the plan's Goal names and which the plan proposed to make WORSE by completing
the command's placeholder.
