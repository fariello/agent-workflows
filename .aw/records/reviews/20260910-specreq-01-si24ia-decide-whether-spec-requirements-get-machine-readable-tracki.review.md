THE FRAMING IS RIGHT AND I DID NOT WEAKEN IT. A decision plan is the correct response to an open question
whose requester said "definitely not sure"; the refusal to build anything is principled rather than timid;
E-04's insistence on recommending rather than handing back an option set is the right call; and demanding a
disconfirming case is the mark of an honest survey. Every structural claim re-verified true: `specs.py` at
1066 lines with exactly two incidental `requirement` hits (`:1048`, `:1063`), `validate_spec` at `:211`
checking only status/gate/history/priority/work-kind, the nine-value `SPEC_STATUSES` with no partial state,
`_SPEC_MAP`'s `approved`->ready and `implementing`->active, and `APPROVAL_FLOOR`'s own written admission that
`aw specs` enforces "presence + format + resolvability, NOT semantic verification that the work truly
happened". F-10 verified exactly: six pending plans name `f1sw71` and exclude it, and only this plan carries
`- From-Backlog: f1sw71`. The three named harm cases hold on substance.

WHAT REVIEW CHANGED IS THE SURVEY, WHICH IS THIS PLAN'S ENTIRE VALUE. The measurement does not reproduce, and
the reason is methodological rather than arithmetic: all six greps were anchored on a leading `- ` bullet, so
none could see a spec that states requirements as bare line-start ids. That single blind spot misclassified
the most systematically id'd spec in the tree as prose-only and understated the parsable requirement
population by roughly 110. Separately the decisive cost figure counts specs whose work is finished, inflating
it about 2x. Both corrections point the same way, toward the full mechanism being cheaper than the plan
argues, which is precisely why they cannot be left for the executor to discover: the maintainer is being
asked to weigh a number, and the number was wrong.

ONE CORRECTION IS A SAFETY MATTER, not an accuracy one. The plan's suite baseline names a failing test that
passes, while the real failure walks a gitignored directory of 189 files belonging to another party in a
shared checkout. An executor told to expect a different failure could reasonably "clean up" that directory to
green the suite and destroy a co-worker's work. An explicit prohibition was added in three places.

Nine findings, all FIXED in place, no deferrals and no new open questions. E-item and V-item counts are
unchanged at five each: every fix belonged inside an existing item, and the fourth option the measurement
demands is a costing variant within E-03 rather than new work.
