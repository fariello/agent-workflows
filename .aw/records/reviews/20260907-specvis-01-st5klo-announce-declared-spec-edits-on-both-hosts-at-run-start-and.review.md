WHAT THIS ROUND CHANGED ABOUT THE PLAN'S SHAPE. It went from three gaps to one gap plus a regression
guard, and its centre of gravity moved from E-02 to E-03. That is a large correction, and it is worth
being precise about what it does and does not mean. It does NOT mean the plan was unnecessary: the
