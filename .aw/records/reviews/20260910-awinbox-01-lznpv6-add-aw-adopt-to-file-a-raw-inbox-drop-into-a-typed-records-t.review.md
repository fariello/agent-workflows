## Round 1

Reviewed at HEAD `dc7f816e`. Structural preflight `aw ipd lint --phase author` conformed (clean, 0 findings)
before semantic review, and `--phase review-finalize` conformed after the revisions. The plan carries no
`- Blocks-Release:`, correctly: backlog `lsztiu` carries none.

DISCLOSURE: authored in the same repository and by the same model family as the plan, so treat this as a
near-self-review and worth less than an independent one.

THE DESIGN IS SOUND AND ITS SHARPEST INSTINCT WAS RIGHT. Every reusable primitive the plan names verifies by
symbol; `aw research new`'s flag surface is exactly as described; the leak sanitizer's two-tier `fail`/`warn`
posture is real and documented where claimed; no `adopt` verb exists. The author's own ADDED constraint,
never adopt an id6 found in the body, is the most valuable thing in the plan, `AGENTS.md` backs it verbatim,
and it is correctly promoted to its own E-item. The three maintainer decisions are implemented as decided
rather than re-litigated, which is the right posture for a plan whose design was already settled.

WHAT REVIEW CHANGED IS THE MECHANICS, AND THREE OF THE CORRECTIONS WOULD EACH HAVE DEFEATED A SAFETY PROPERTY
THE PLAN EXISTS TO DELIVER. The id6 guard is written against the wrong front-matter dialect and would not see
the case it will meet first. The leak gate names a scan mode that cannot see an untracked file, so the naive
implementation passes everything silently. And the override's recording requirement, implemented naively,
would copy the leaked string into a tracked artifact and make that record permanently fail `aw sanitize`.
None of these is a design flaw; all three are the difference between a gate and the appearance of one.

THE INBOX MEASUREMENTS WERE WRONG IN EVERY PARTICULAR, and one correction is a new hazard rather than an
accuracy nit: an inbox file may ALREADY be adopted. The `awmetastore` topic is a complete six-file adopted
set whose `gemini31prohigh` variant still sits in the inbox, and re-adopting it would mint a second id6 for
content that already has one, which `aw check` cannot detect because it is not a collision.

THE SIZE EXCEPTION WAS ASSESSED AND ACCEPTED. It is claimed for cohesion rather than volume, the per-item
right-sizing diagnostics all pass, and the argument holds on its own terms: the destructive step is what
makes the other properties load-bearing, so a partial ship is a net hazard rather than a partial gain.

Eight findings, all FIXED in place, no deferrals. OQ-02's precondition is now ANSWERED (which raises its cost
and is why it stays open for the maintainer) and OQ-03 is re-grounded on corrected figures. E-item and V-item
counts are unchanged at seven each.
