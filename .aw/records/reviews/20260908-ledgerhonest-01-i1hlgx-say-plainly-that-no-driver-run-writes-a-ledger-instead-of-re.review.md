WHAT THIS ROUND CHANGED ABOUT THE PLAN'S SHAPE. Its REACH, not its correctness, and in the direction that
makes the edit smaller. The plan fixed one leaf on the reasonable assumption that its target was one
branch; measurement found ten leaves printing the identical sentence and, more usefully, found that seven
of them already share the helper that emits it. So the wording moves into `_resolve_or_error`, the three
inline duplicates route through it, and coverage goes from one tenth to complete while the diff shrinks
from a per-leaf survey to one function plus three call sites. That created one new hazard worth its own
finding, since six of the ten callers are action verbs for which "cannot verify" would be false, so the
shared core must stay factual. The rest is measurement hygiene: a finding aimed at the wrong five
symbols, a suite baseline wrong for the third plan in a row with the same wrongly-named expected failure,
and a dependency described as queued that has already shipped.
