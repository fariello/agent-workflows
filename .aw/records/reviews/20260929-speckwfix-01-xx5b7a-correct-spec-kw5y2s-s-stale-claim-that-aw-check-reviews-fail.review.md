WHAT REVIEW FOUND IS TWO THINGS, one mechanical and one about completeness.

THE MECHANICAL ONE WOULD HAVE MISFIRED SILENTLY. E-01(e) prescribes `rg -n "ARTIFACT_TYPES
only|RecordClass only"` and its `Expected outcome` says four hits. The spec's cells read
`` | `ARTIFACT_TYPES` only (NEW to `RecordClass`) | `` with the identifier BACKTICKED, so that pattern
returns ZERO under `rg` and under `grep` alike, verified both ways. Zero against an expectation of
four reads as "the rows were already corrected", which is false; the likely outcomes are a spurious
stop or a skipped table edit. A recipe that silently returns zero is worse than one that errors.

THE COMPLETENESS ONE IS THE SUBSTANTIVE FINDING. The plan widened the defect from one line to four
table cells and argued explicitly, citing `olkeju`'s review of the sibling spec, that partial
correction is the failure mode to avoid. That reasoning is right and it does not go far enough: the
identical assertion appears in THREE further places the plan does not name. Section 1.2's "Drift and
Inconsistency" bullet offers as its examples "`backlog` in `ARTIFACT_TYPES` vs. missing in
`RecordClass`, or `reviews` in `RecordClass` vs. `ARTIFACT_TYPES`" - both false at HEAD - in the
PROBLEM STATEMENT, which a graduating Set reads before Section 3.2. Section 5.1 items 1 and 2 still
say `reviews` "is gained" and the module "Gains `backlog`, `roadmaps`, and `other`". Correcting
Section 3.2 alone relocates the false premise rather than removing it.
