WHAT REVIEW ADDED. Three things the plan did not have, each found by running the candidate patch
rather than reading the plan. FIRST, E-01's census instruction named the `id()`-dedup idiom while
asking the executor to confirm counts that are only reachable by NOT deduplicating, so a faithful
executor would have reported a contradiction against two correct findings (PR-201). SECOND, the
override reaches the eight MISSING-FLAG leaves and absorbs an undeclared flag's VALUE as a positional,
changing the refusal message; the end-to-end refusal survives, but only because `parse_args` errors on
any leftover, and nothing pinned that (PR-202). THIRD, a third honest limit exists and is the one an
operator meets first: a flag before an intermediate GROUP token still fails, so this plan's own title
word "anywhere" overpromises and the CHANGELOG would have inherited that (PR-203).

I also measured something STRONGER than the plan claims for itself: the WHOLE default suite passes
under the candidate override at `3246 passed, 2 skipped`, identical to the unpatched baseline, not
merely the six modules F-8 names. That is recorded as F-13 with the honest caveat that it was taken on
3.14 alone and therefore does not substitute for V-07's multi-interpreter run.
