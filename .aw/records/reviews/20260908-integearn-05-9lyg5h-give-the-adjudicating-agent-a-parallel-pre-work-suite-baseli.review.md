CONFIRMED SOUND, and worth stating because the plan's core premise and its central discipline both
survive. Re-verified: the post-work suite really does run exactly ONCE, after the work, on both hosts
(`oc_runipd.py:6669`, `agy_runipd.py:3724`), and nothing persists a prior result, so the agent really
cannot know whether a failure pre-existed its work. F-1 holds exactly as written and is the reason
this plan should exist. The maintainer's not-a-gate ruling is correctly identified as the binding
constraint, and E-05/V-05 are the right shape for proving a NEGATIVE property (a test that a verdict
is honored identically with and without the id present), which is a sharper instrument than a code
comment. The commit-pinning requirement is correct and its reasoning (a mid-turn merge would make a
branch-pinned baseline describe a different tree) is sound. The absence-is-not-empty rule (F-9, E-04)
correctly generalizes the inversion `daexj1` E-02 guards on the other side. The concurrency argument
is honest: ~81 seconds against turns of minutes to hours genuinely does make the wall-clock cost
approximately zero, and review's own measurement (54s for a full suite) supports it. The split from
`daexj1` was also checked rather than accepted: `daexj1` really does carry ten E-items across eight
files, its review really does record `REVIEWED - OPEN QUESTIONS` with one advisory density note and
no structural error, and its only blocking question really was OQ-02, which the maintainer resolved.
So the plan's claim that `daexj1`'s `no-go` is stale rather than a size verdict is correct.

WHAT THIS ROUND CHANGED ABOUT THE PLAN'S SHAPE. Two of the three BLOCKERs pull in opposite directions
and that is the most useful thing to report. PR-501 makes the plan SMALLER: the divergence-neutralizing
half, which the plan calls "the highest risk in the plan and the reason it exists separately", is
measurably unnecessary today, so E-01's honest output is a measurement plus a regression test rather
than a mechanism. PR-503 makes the remaining half MORE DANGEROUS than the plan understood: the
baseline checkout, described throughout as read-only and harmless, was prescribed in a way that
deletes the agent's committed work. A plan whose stated worst case was "making the runner more
fragile in exchange for better information" in fact had unrecoverable data loss on its default path.
Both were found by execution, not inspection, and neither is visible in the prose.
