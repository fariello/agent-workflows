## Round 1

Reviewed at HEAD `0a073ea9`. `aw ipd lint --phase author --agent` CONFORMING before semantic review and
`--phase review-finalize --agent` CONFORMING after every revision. Claims were re-measured by IMPORTING
the modules, CALLING the engine functions both ways, and RUNNING the suite in an isolated worktree.

THE PLAN'S DIAGNOSIS IS SOUND AND ITS SELF-CORRECTION IS EXEMPLARY, which is worth stating before the
findings. Re-verified independently: minting really is per-tree at every site; `_check_identity_slots`
really returns ZERO findings for a record pair that both DECLARES and SLOTS the same id6 (called
directly with a synthetic pair); the retired filter really does hide a terminal-involving collision; and
`aw check research` really does exit 0 reporting `errors 0 warnings 0` over a tree containing a real
collision. The plan's second history entry, which caught that its own E-04 had duplicated `76w6mq`'s
work and rewrote it into a consumption check with negative-proof validation, is the right instinct
applied rigorously, and it is preserved intact.

THE FINDINGS BELOW ARE ALMOST ENTIRELY ABOUT UNMEASURED CONSEQUENCE AND CORPUS DRIFT, not about wrong
reasoning. Three matter enough to change what execution does.

FIRST, E-01's CALL-SITE LIST OMITTED THE TREE THAT OWNS THE LIVE COLLISION (PR-001). A grep for
`generate_id6` finds ELEVEN mint sites across EIGHT modules, not the six across five the plan named.
`research_cmd.py:183`/`:240` and `set_records.py:275` were absent from the E-01 list AND from
`- Scope-Paths:`, and `ipd_authoring.py:564` was unnamed. The sharpest consequence: every non-plan
participant in the measured `uyeko5` collision is a RESEARCH record, so the single mint path most
implicated by the motivating defect was the one path this plan had no authority to touch. An executor
following the plan literally would have globalized five modules, left research and set-records minting
per-tree, and reported the item complete.

SECOND, E-05's CENTRAL EXPECTATION IS MEASURABLY FALSE (PR-002). It required that "no other rule's count
changed", but `check_collisions` emits THREE rules from ONE enumeration. Measured by calling it both
ways: `check.id6-collision` 1 to 2, `check.setid-collision` 39 to 86, `check.id6-identity-slot` 0 to 2.
So widening the enumeration wholesale ships a +47 setid batch that this plan's own F-9 explicitly
declined to own, plus two FALSE POSITIVES: walkthroughs `zpbx7o` and `y5od1h` carry their plan's id6 in
the filename slot while declaring no `- Id:`, which is the documented walkthrough convention and exactly
the mass-flagging class the plan warns against elsewhere. This is the `76w6mq` E-06 batch warning coming
true with a concrete number.

THIRD, A THIRD UNBOUNDED IDENTITY READER EXISTS AND IT IS THE ONE E-01 BUILDS ON (PR-003).
`status_set._ID_RE` is multiline-unbounded and matches a `- Id:` inside a fenced code block; it backs
`inventory_all_artifacts`, i.e. the global mint set E-01 consumes. `76w6mq`'s declared scope covers
`selectors.py` and `check_engine.py` and NOT this reader, so one unbounded reader survives even after
that plan lands. The direction of the error is what makes this tractable rather than fatal: an
over-collecting reader yields a conservative SUPERSET, which is harmless for minting and wrong only for
checking. New E-07 records that, comments the helper, and hands the finding off rather than making this
the third plan editing an identity reader, which is precisely the drift `cqytxf` warns about.

THE CORPUS ALSO MOVED UNDER THE PLAN IN THREE WAYS THAT INVALIDATE ITS STATED EVIDENCE. The motivating
asymmetry has INVERTED: `aw check all` now reports ONE id6-collision, not ZERO, because a new
research-to-research pair is visible while the executed-plan pair is still hidden. A THIRD file now
quotes `- Id: uyeko5` (research report `takpys` at `:141`, in a fenced block, created after authoring),
which makes the defect a recurring generator of phantom identities rather than a historical accident and
is the strongest available argument for the parser bound. And EVERY baseline figure was stale, including
a claimed known test failure that does not exist.

No BLOCKER. Nine findings, all FIXED, none deferred. OQ-02 remains open by design as a maintainer
cost/silence tradeoff; that is the correct disposition, since E-06 is written so the cheapest honest
option is a legitimate resolution. The `- Blocks-Release: next` gate is correctly inherited from backlog
`wx95o4` (which carries the same gate) and was preserved through the status transition.
