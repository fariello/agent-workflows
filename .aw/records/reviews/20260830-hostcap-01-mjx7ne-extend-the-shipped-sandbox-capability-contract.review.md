## Round 1

All claims verified at HEAD `f7a4d53df2209915d8954c51cc698e29db63432e`, working tree clean.

The plan's central judgement is correct and well evidenced: `host_sandbox_profile.HostSandboxCapabilities`
really is the right owner, the skill-delivery registry really is a different concern, and
`RUN-HOST-CAPABILITY` really is a live cross-plan contract that `7f7782` cites seven times and that greps
to zero in the package. The supersession of `a54m79` is sound and its substance is genuinely preserved.

Two problems dominate. First, the plan reintroduces the exact `## Workflow history` ordering defect that
PR-006 fixed on its own predecessor, so it arrived failing `aw check plans` (PR-001, fixed). Second, and
not repairable inside this plan: two of the three capabilities it adds describe host ENFORCEMENT that does
not exist anywhere in this repository, so their probes have nothing to attempt. The plan's own
attempt-not-inspect discipline therefore cannot be satisfied for them, and the two honest options differ in
scope enough that the maintainer must choose (PR-002, escalated as blocking OQ-03).

A related irony worth recording: the module the plan correctly chooses as owner is not itself faithful to
the attempt-not-inspect rule it publishes, since it asserts two capabilities from `host == "opencode"` with
only a comment as evidence.
