## Round 1

Reviewed at HEAD `fb137c31` in an isolated review lane. The plan file was committed and byte-identical
to the lane input (`diff` reports no difference), so no pre-review snapshot was needed. Structural
preflight `aw ipd lint --phase author --agent` reported `conforming` (exit 0, zero findings) BEFORE
semantic review; `--phase review-finalize --agent` reports `conforming` after revision, including the
new `E-07`/`V-07` pair.

THE PLAN'S CENTRAL PREMISE IS CORRECT AND I RE-MEASURED ALL OF IT. The whole design rests on one claim:
that emitting the runner's OWN canonical vocabulary needs no spec amendment and no new `lifecycle_style`
row. Measured independently: `runner_shutdown.KNOWN_ITEM_STATUSES` has 28 members; composing
`LEGACY_INTEGRATION_STATUS_ALIASES` then `canonical_terminal_status` yields 17 distinct words; the
longest is `already-landed` at exactly 14 characters; there are ZERO truncation collisions at every
width from 7 through 14; and all 17 resolve through `lifecycle_style.resolve(FAMILY_RUNNER_ITEM, ...)`
to a non-`unknown` stage with no diagnostic. Every number in the plan matched. The legacy alias keys
resolve cleanly too.

THE DEFECTS ARE REAL AND USER-VISIBLE. F-2 reproduced: `resolve(FAMILY_RUNNER_ITEM, "merging")` and
`"done"` both return stage `unknown`, color 244, glyph `?`, with a diagnostic, so the naive substitution
really would regress the display. F-3 reproduced on all six words, including the one a user can see
without any conversion: `queued` is painted 220, the amber this spec reserves for ACTIVE work, while the
resolver gives it 45 (`ready`). F-4 reproduced end to end and it is the sharpest defect in the plan:
five statuses reach the old `else` branch and are stored PRE-TRUNCATED, and because the fragment is what
the filter compares against, `--run-status already-landed` matches NOTHING today (verified by driving
`parse_run_status_filters` and `matches_run_status` against a map holding `already`). F-5's two
collisions (`integra`, `merge-r`) reproduced exactly. F-6 held: no assignment of `"merging"` exists
anywhere in the package outside `attention.py` itself, and it is not a member of the owner enum. F-7's
mechanism reproduced: the two VS-bearing graphemes have `len()` 2 and `visible_width` 1, so the existing
`len()`-based pad really does break the moment a glyph enters the cell. F-8's four dependents all exist,
and `RUN_SORT_ORDER` really is not spec-governed. F-10's trap is real: the 214 and 40 escapes in
`tests/test_attention.py` belong to the OQ-count column, not to lifecycle.

WHAT REVIEW FOUND. One BLOCKER, and it is a severity change rather than a new discovery: the plan
already knew about `runner_shared.format_slated_artifacts_table` (F-9) and classified it LOW, deferring
it as tidy-up to be filed as a follow-up. That classification is wrong, and the reason is mechanical.
That function does not merely duplicate the mapping; it passes its OWN hand-built `run_map` directly into
`attention.render_table(..., runs_mode=True, run_map=run_map)`, the exact function E-04 converts. Two of
the five words it synthesizes are `done` (unmapped) and `queued` (used as a catch-all for statuses that
are not queued). So the moment E-04 lands, every `done` row in the runner's pre-flight table prints `?`
in gray. The plan would have shipped a user-visible regression in a surface it does not otherwise touch
and filed a backlog item about it. That is now E-07 and the fence gained `runner_shared.py`. I kept the
genuinely open part of the original deferral (whether the runner should build a `run_map` at all)
deferred, since no measured defect stands behind it once the words are right.

Three further findings concern the test blast radius, which the plan understates in a way that makes one
of its own instructions impossible to follow honestly: E-05 says update four assertions, but the `--runs`
test drives a HAND-WRITTEN `run_map` containing `done`, a value the post-E-01 map can never produce, so
the fixture must be re-pointed before any assertion can be re-spelled. The real census is ten assertions
plus two fixtures, spread across two tests.
