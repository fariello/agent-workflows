## Round 1

All claims verified at HEAD `9089f2cd` against SHIPPED code, by reading the real functions and by
probing `runner_profiles` live. `aw ipd lint` conforming at `--phase author` and again at
`--phase review-finalize`. The target plan was committed and unchanged, so the pre-review snapshot was
correctly skipped.

CONTEXT THAT SHAPED THE REVIEW: this plan was authored one day before it was reviewed, and in that
window three of its six `runprofile` siblings executed (`3cm15q`, `ygzq71`, and earlier `f2mrsw` and
`p0l1to`). So the mechanism it extends stopped being a planned design and became working code, every
line number it cites drifted, and two of its cross-plan instructions went stale. That is not the
plan's fault, but it is the reviewer's job to catch, because an executor following the authored text
would edit a plan in `executed/` and would misread the argv seam.

WHAT THE PLAN GOT RIGHT, and it is the substance: the goal is well motivated and traceable to a
maintainer decision; the choice of a profile REFERENCE over an inline model is correct and its
justification (an inline model would be the first field to escape the schema's validation) is exactly
right given `FORBIDDEN_PROFILE_KEYS`; the insistence that absent must mean "same as executor" rather
than false or null correctly reuses the `validate` tri-state discipline; the identification of the
freeze as the load-bearing part is right; F-6's self-correction of an earlier wrong cost claim is the
kind of honesty that makes a plan trustworthy; and V-04's instinct that the no-op default is the test
a reviewer should distrust most is precisely the right instinct. It is also right that this is a
call-site change rather than a new subprocess path.

THE ONE THING THAT WOULD HAVE BROKEN THE DEFAULT CASE: the plan inherits `3cm15q`'s "four turn kinds"
vocabulary and describes the verifier as "the independent fresh-session verifier". At the code level
that phrase does not identify the verifier. `fresh_session` is also true for every ISOLATED turn, by a
deliberate decision taken after lanes were lost to session collisions, and isolation is the DEFAULT.
An implementation faithful to the plan's own wording would therefore have routed nearly every execute
turn through the verifier's model, and would have done so while passing every test the plan asked for.
