`init` -- so the backlog item's disk-re-hash detector really would never fire, and the plan's decision
to move the retry inside `commit_isolated` is correct at its root. F-4 holds (the mutating hooks do fire
on the agent-write paths; the exclude regex covers only research and `.aw/system/`). F-5 holds
(`offer_commit` makes exactly one attempt, resets our paths, returns an error). F-7 holds and is the
reason this shipped: there is NO hook-rejection test at all, because `tests/support.init_repo` is a bare
`git init` with no hook, and the module is indeed `tests/support.py` not `tests/support/__init__.py` as
the item said. F-8 holds (whole-file rstrip leaves per-line trailing whitespace). F-9 re-measured and
still true at a larger corpus: 0 trailing-whitespace lines and 0 two-space hard breaks across 1108
tracked `.aw/records` markdown files, so both the preventive framing and the "hard breaks cost nothing"
claim are evidence-backed. F-11 and F-12 verified as described. The `exactly once, never a loop` bound
is right for the measured reason the plan gives.

WHAT THIS ROUND CHANGED ABOUT THE PLAN'S SHAPE. The diagnosis was accepted wholesale; the MECHANISM
needed three additions, and the pattern connecting them is that the plan reasoned carefully about the
defect and much less carefully about its own remedy's side effects. Each of PR-001, PR-002 and PR-003
appeared only when the proposed retry was actually built and run: one leaves the tree dirty on the path
just committed, one fails on the commonest shape of the defect, one crashes on the finalize caller. A
plan whose validation section demands pasted evidence for everything nonetheless asserted its own
mechanism's behavior without executing it once. The second theme is narrower and recurs three times on
the normalization half: the plan treated `atomic_write` as if it wrote only artifact markdown, when it
also writes JSON, also writes the tree the hooks preserve verbatim, and is not even the path plans go
through. Both themes are now closed with items and negative proofs rather than prose.
