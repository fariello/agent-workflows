### Scope and Verification Summary

1. **Missing Research Front Matter Schemas in Section 6.3 (`research_contract.FRONTMATTER_FIELDS` Compliance) (F-89)**: Identified that Section 6.3 provided front matter YAML schemas for specs and review records, but completely omitted schemas for research artifacts (`.research-report.md` and `.reconciliation-report.md`). In `agent_workflows/research_contract.py`, `validate_frontmatter()` strictly enforces `FRONTMATTER_FIELDS = ('id', 'created', 'set', 'order', 'topic', 'model', 'kind', 'status', 'outcome', 'summary', 'consumed-by')` using lowercase keys and unhyphenated `created: YYYYMMDD`. Without explicit normative schemas, runner models generating research reports would borrow spec front matter (`- Date:`, `- Id:`), immediately failing `validate_frontmatter()`. Added normative schemas for Candidate Research Reports and Reconciled Research Reports to Section 6.3.
2. **Similarities and Differences Matrix Schema and `review_findings.py` Invariant Alignment (F-90)**: Discovered that Table 6.2 places the Similarities and Differences Matrix in `.aw/records/reviews/` with filename `YYYYMMDD-<setid>-50-<id6>-matrix.reconciliation.review.md`. In `agent_workflows/review_findings.py`, `parse_review_document()` parses every `*.review.md` file in `.aw/records/reviews/`, requiring 5 metadata fields (`subject-id`, `subject-type`, `reviewed-at`, `reviewer`, `verdict`) and a `## Round <N>` section header; missing fields trigger diagnostics and cause `check.review-dangling` / `check.review-format` failures during `aw check`. Added the normative YAML front matter schema for the Similarities and Differences Matrix in Section 6.3 with `- Subject-Id: <synthesized_spec_id6>`, `- Subject-Type: spec`, `- Reviewer: reconciliation/<synthesizer_model>`, `- Verdict: REVIEWED - MATERIAL CHANGES APPLIED`, `- Matrix-Candidates: [<id6_1>, ...]`, and structured the content under `## Round 1\n\n### Synthesis Similarities and Differences Matrix`.
3. **Review Tree `SUBJECT_TYPES` Closed Vocabulary Gap for Research Critiques (F-91)**: Identified that when research candidate reports undergo iterative refinement, Table 6.2 and Section 4.3 route critique records to `.aw/records/reviews/`. However, in `agent_workflows/review_findings.py:73`, `SUBJECT_TYPES` is strictly closed to `("ipd", "spec")`, causing `check.review-subject-type-unknown` (`REV-M102`), and `check_engine._review_subject_id_sets` only resolves IPD and Spec IDs, causing dangling subject errors. Neither Section 2.2 nor Section 11 documented this closed vocabulary gap. Added the required extension to Section 2.2 Table and Section 11 Phase 1, and updated Section 6.3 review critique schema to note `- Subject-Type: spec | research`.
4. **Single-Participant Research Finalization Kind & Contract Contradiction (F-92)**: Resolved a contract contradiction in Section 4.5 FR-18B, which stated that all finalized research reports are written as `reconciliation-report`. In a single-participant research run (`N = 1`), reconciliation is skipped (Flowchart 7.2, Section 4.3.1) and findings represent one model; per Decision D177 and `research_contract.py`, labeling single-model research as `reconciliation-report` falsely indicates multi-model reconciliation. Corrected FR-18B and Table 3.4 so single-participant research finalizes as kind `research-report`, reserving `reconciliation-report` for multi-participant synthesis.
5. **State Diagram 7.3 Missing Synthesis Crash Transitions & Finalization Refusal (F-93)**: Corrected State Diagram 7.3 to model execution failures during synthesis and synthesis refinement (transitions to `Failed: Synthesizer Error / Worker Crash`), added `BaselineFrozen --> Refused: Dangling Profile / Delimiter Collision (Exit 2)` (FR-32, FR-36), and added `Finalizing --> BlockedFindings: Unresolved Critical/Major Findings (Exit 1)` to model the refusal branch when `finalize` is invoked without `--allow-unresolved-findings`.
6. **Table 6.2 Research Artifact Gaps and Informal NN Notation (F-94)**: Added missing rows in Table 6.2 for `4. Revised Candidate (Research)` (`cand-i2`) and `6. Reconciliation (Research)` (active multi-participant report in flat hot root `.aw/records/research/`). Corrected informal non-digit notation `0X` in row 2 context packets to standard pattern `YYYYMMDD-<setid>-NN-<id6>-context.<ace>.<model>.prompt.md (NN in 01..09)`.
7. **CLI Options Synchronization across Spec and Research Commands in Table 3.4 (F-95)**: Added `--allow-partial-roster` to `aw research flow new` (FR-30 parity); added `--investigation-mode parallel|decomposed` and `--topic <str>` to `aw research flow prepare` (needed to prepare decomposed prompts); added `--allow-unverified` (cited in outcome description) as well as `--budget`, `--turn-timeout`, and `--max-retries` to `aw research flow reconcile`; added `--budget`, `--turn-timeout`, and `--max-retries` to `aw specs flow merge`; and added `--title <str>` to `aw specs flow prepare` and `from-spec`.
8. **Acceptance Test Matrix Coverage Expansion (F-96)**: Added acceptance tests `AT-41` (`aw specs flow config` hierarchy inspection and user-local setting isolation), `AT-42` (`aw research flow finalize` and `reconcile` refusal with exit 1 on unverified citations without `--allow-unverified`), `AT-43` (single-participant research run finalizing to `reference/` as `research-report` without reconciliation consensus fabrication), and `AT-44` (hierarchical two-pass synthesis turn bounds accounting under FR-34), expanding audited scope to `AT-01..AT-44`.
