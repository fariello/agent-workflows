THE HIGHEST-VALUE FINDING IS THAT E-04 AS AUTHORED WOULD HAVE ENCODED A RULE THE PLAN DENIES ONE ITEM
EARLIER. E-04 offered two closure formulations and asserted they were "equivalent for this purpose".
They are not. `DRIVER_GENERATIONS` maps a module BASENAME to a generation, so its values are exactly
the four hosts that HAVE a runner module; `_GENERATION_HOSTS` additionally carries `scripted`. A
DESCRIPTOR-ONLY host therefore FAILS the authored conjunction and PASSES the single-predicate
alternative. That matters because a descriptor-only host is legitimate by this plan's own design - it
is the entire reason E-01 keeps `driver_path=None` legal, and the plan's own "Project conventions
discovered" section says so explicitly - so an executor picking the first-listed formulation would
have shipped a registry closure test that refuses the very host shape the plan spends three paragraphs
protecting. E-04 now mandates the single predicate and forbids the conjunction by name.

THE SECOND FINDING IS A SHARED-CHECKOUT HAZARD IN THE VALIDATION METHOD, NOT IN THE DESIGN. The plan
required three deliberate-failure demonstrations and instructed the executor to obtain them by editing
`oc_runipd.py` and `agy_runipd.py` with a hand-rolled backup and restore, twice, each time running a
suite that takes 40-plus seconds. In a shared checkout a restore-from-backup silently reverts whatever
a co-worker wrote to those files in the interval, which is precisely the loss AGENTS.md's
shared-checkout rule exists to prevent, and the plan's own gate elsewhere warns that this is a shared
checkout. All three demonstrations were re-derived at review by in-memory patching and all three work:
the relocation via a pytest plugin wrapping `initialize_run_core`, the omission by popping the kwarg,
and the registry closure via `mock.patch.object(..., create=True)`. The plan now demands that method.

THE THIRD FINDING IS THE ONE THE PLAN COULD NOT HAVE FOUND BY RE-READING ITSELF: the sibling parameter
`labels` carries the identical hazard, on the field F-04 identifies as the one currently carrying
attribution. `labels: HostLabels | None = None` is the same keyword-only optional-default shape in the
same parameter list, and `driver.id` is computed FROM it with a bare-`host`-string fallback. So the
plan as authored fixed the half F-04 measures as NOT load-bearing and left defaulted the half that IS.
Measured: dropping `labels` from every caller leaves the suite at its unchanged baseline; both real
hosts survive because the `host == "agy"`/`"oc"` fallback happens to reproduce their ids, and a third
host does not. Fixed here as new E-06 rather than filed, for the reasons recorded as decision D-3.

TWO SMALLER CORRECTIONS OF FACT ARE WORTH NAMING because both were overclaims in the direction of
under-counting. The plan says three `initialize_run_core` call sites and there are four

the evaluator now returns zero drifts for this plan and `aw check` is back to 4 findings, none of them
this plan or this spec. The pre-review count was 5; one of the five (`check.scope-drift` on

lower rather than equal. FINAL GATES: `aw ipd lint --phase review-finalize --agent` exit 0
