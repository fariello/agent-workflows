### Scope and Verification Summary

1. **Severity Vocabulary Alignment across Spec Tables, Rules, Diagrams, and Transcripts (F-113)**: While Iteration 17 updated Section 4.3 FR-07 to cite `review_findings.FINDING_COLUMNS` and closed `SEVERITIES` (`blocker`, `high`, `medium`, `low`), non-normative severity terms (`critical`, `major`, `minor`) remained pervasive throughout Table 3.4 (lines 200, 204), Section 4.6 FR-20 (line 354), Section 7.3 State Diagram (line 1016), Section 9 Acceptance Tests (AT-04 line 1086, AT-06 line 1088, AT-36 line 1118), and Section 13 Worked Transcripts (13.1 lines 1263, 1269; 13.2 lines 1294, 1295, 1296, 1307; 13.3 lines 1327, 1328, 1329). Emitting review files or transcripts with non-normative severities causes `review_findings.parse_review_text` to fail with diagnostic `REV-P002`. Additionally, Table 3.4 listed a phantom `--allow-unresolved-minor` option, whereas `low` and `medium` severities are advisory-only per FR-07 and do not gate finalization. Harmonized all occurrences of `critical` and `major` to `blocker` and `high`, and `minor` to `low` or `medium` advisory findings, and eliminated `--allow-unresolved-minor` from Table 3.4.
2. **Flowcharts 7.1 and 7.2 Missing Cap-with-Findings Halt Decision Diamonds and Nodes (F-114)**: Flowcharts 7.1 and 7.2 depicted refinement decision diamonds unconditionally transitioning to `Ready`, `Synthesis`, or `Finalization` on `Yes`, completely omitting the halted cap state mandated by FR-11 and shown in State Diagram 7.3 and Transcript 13.3 (`cp73m9`). If refinement or synthesis refinement reaches cap with unresolved blocker or high findings, execution must halt with exit code 1 in state `capped_with_findings`, rather than proceeding to synthesis or finalization. Additionally, terminal node O in Flowchart 7.1 routed to node P which unconditionally displayed `/spec-review`, even when finalization places the spec in `draft/` (which outputs `aw specs set to-review <id6>`). Updated Flowcharts 7.1 and 7.2 with explicit decision diamonds and branches to halted states (`CappedHalted`, `SynthCappedHalted`, `RC_solo_halt`, `RC_multi_halt`, `RM_halt`) with exit 1 and `capped_with_findings`/`unverified` state, and branched node P based on lifecycle destination (`to-review/` vs `draft/`).
3. **CLI Options Asymmetry: Missing Title on Research and Allow-Unresolved-Findings on Merge/Reconcile (F-115)**: In Table 3.4, `aw research flow new` and `aw research flow prepare` omitted `--title <str>`, preventing operators from providing explicit report titles at initialization (unlike `specs flow new` and `research flow reconcile`). Furthermore, `specs flow new`, `from-spec`, and `merge`, as well as `research flow new` and `reconcile`, lacked `--allow-unresolved-findings`, preventing operators from forcing a draft synthesis/finalization when refinement caps are reached with unresolved issues. Added `--title <str> (optional)` to `research flow new` and `prepare`. Added `--allow-unresolved-findings (flag)` to `specs flow new`, `from-spec`, `merge`, and `research flow new`, `reconcile`.
4. **Resume CLI Options Incompleteness: Missing Timeout and Retry Overrides (F-116)**: When resuming an interrupted, timed-out, or failed run, operators could adjust `--refine-cap`, `--synth-refine-cap`, and `--budget`, but could not override `--turn-timeout` or `--max-retries`. If a run was interrupted or failed due to a transient network timeout or worker hang requiring a higher timeout threshold (e.g. increasing from 600s to 1200s), the resume command lacked options to adjust these thresholds, forcing repetitive timeouts. Added `--turn-timeout <int> (optional new timeout)` and `--max-retries <int> (optional new max retries)` to both `aw specs flow resume` and `aw research flow resume` in Table 3.4.
5. **Missing Research Reconciliation Evaluation Relationship in ERD (F-117)**: The Section 6.1 ERD modeled `SYNTHESIS_ARTIFACT ||--o{ ITERATION_FINDING : evaluates` for Stage 4 spec synthesis refinement, but omitted the corresponding evaluation relationship for Stage 5 research reconciliation refinement (`RECONCILIATION_REPORT ||--o{ ITERATION_FINDING : evaluates`), breaking conceptual symmetry between spec and research refinement flows as defined in Section 4.3.1 (lines 290-292). Added `RECONCILIATION_REPORT ||--o{ ITERATION_FINDING : evaluates` to the Section 6.1 ERD.
6. **State Diagram 7.3 BlockedFindings Terminal Dead-End Recovery Transitions (F-118)**: In State Diagram 7.3, state `BlockedFindings` (resulting from unforced finalization with unresolved blocker/high findings) transitioned directly to `[*]` without documenting recovery paths. An operator halted at `BlockedFindings` can recover by resuming refinement via `aw specs flow resume` with increased caps, or by forcing draft finalization via `aw specs flow finalize --allow-unresolved-findings`. Added recovery transitions from `BlockedFindings` to `Finalizing` (via `aw specs flow finalize --allow-unresolved-findings`) and to `Dispatching` (via `aw specs flow resume`).
7. **TOML Dotted Model Normalization Key Quoting Specification (F-119)**: Line 1192 listed unquoted normalization mappings containing dots (such as `gpt-6.1-sol-medium = "gpt61solmedium"`, `gpt-6.1 = "gpt61"`). In standard TOML, bare keys containing dots are interpreted as table hierarchies (`[gpt-6]` with key `1`). They must be quoted string keys (e.g. `"gpt-6.1" = "gpt61"`) in `research-models.toml`. Clarified that TOML keys containing dots must be quoted string keys in `[normalizations]`.
8. **Acceptance Test Matrix Scope Expansion (F-120)**: Acceptance tests lacked explicit coverage for research title options (`AT-53`), `--allow-unresolved-findings` on merge/reconcile (`AT-54`), closed severity vocabulary conformance on emitted findings (`AT-55`), and flowchart cap-halt decision branching (`AT-56`). Added acceptance tests `AT-53` through `AT-56` to Section 9, updated Section 6.3 review front matter schema scope to `AT-01..AT-56`, and updated Section 12.2 self-check item.
