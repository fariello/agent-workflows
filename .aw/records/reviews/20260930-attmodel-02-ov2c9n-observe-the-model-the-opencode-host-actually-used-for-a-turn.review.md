THE PLAN'S CORE JUDGEMENT IS RIGHT AND ITS HOMEWORK WAS REAL. It correctly refuses the backlog item's stated
mechanism (the `--format json` stream carries no model, which I corroborated structurally against
`runner_shared`'s own recorded census), correctly refuses the SQLite route on three defensible grounds,
correctly identifies `opencode export` as the host's own supported interface, correctly homes the reader in
`oc_runipd` with injection into `runner_shared` (the seam and the injection pattern both exist exactly as
described: `execute_item_core` resolves a dozen names off `driver_module` and reads `cost`/`tokens` from the
finished log immediately after resolving the session id), and correctly separates the observation from Order
01's frozen request rather than collapsing them. Three of its five host-surface premises re-executed
verbatim under 1.18.33: stdout/stderr separation, `info.model.{id,providerID,variant}` presence with
`variant` optional, and a nonzero exit with `Error: Session not found: <id>` for a bogus id.

TWO PREMISES DID NOT HOLD, AND BOTH WERE FOUND ONLY BY EXECUTING THE HOST RATHER THAN READING THE PLAN.

First and most serious, `info.model` IS SESSION-CURRENT RATHER THAN TURN-SPECIFIC (PR-001). One session, two
turns, two different models: the export reported only the SECOND, while the per-message `modelID` values
retained both in order. The plan's title, Goal and E-03 all claim to record what "a turn" ran, and the
mechanism cannot deliver that grain. This is not a contrived case: `oc_runipd.run_opencode` resolves a
non-isolated turn's session from `state["session_id"]` or `state["set_sessions"][setid]` and rotates only at
`options["max_items_per_session"]` (default 4), and a review sweep deliberately shares ONE session across the
whole sweep. The plan remains sound because its chosen seam reads inside the window where the answer is still
this turn's; what was wrong was the CLAIM, so the fix narrows the claim, forces a mechanism-naming source
label, pins it behaviorally, and records the rejected alternatives in a new OQ-04.

Second, THE EXPORT SPLITS THE IDENTIFIER THE LAUNCH FLAG JOINS (PR-002), and the consequence is an inverted
feature. A turn launched with `--model opencode/nemotron-3.5-lightning-free` exports
`id="nemotron-3.5-lightning-free"` with `providerID="opencode"`. A reader recording bare `id`, compared by
Order 03 against Order 01's frozen `model`, would report a SUBSTITUTION on every single oc run. The plan's
headline capability is making a real substitution visible; unfixed, it would have made substitution
indistinguishable from spelling and fired constantly. Note the second measured case defeats the obvious
workaround: `id` itself contains a slash (`its_direct/pt3-claude-opus-5-1m-us` under `providerID="uri"`), so
the split cannot be undone by guessing a one-segment prefix.

A THIRD CLASS OF FINDING IS WORTH NAMING SEPARATELY BECAUSE IT RECURS IN THIS SET: two mechanisms the plan
cited as live ENFORCEMENT do not exist. `tests/test_runner_shared.py::NoRunnerImportTests` has zero matches
under `tests/`, and `tests/test_run_flag_surface.py` was deleted outright in commit `19313eed` (4,863 lines).
Both underlying RULES are still correct and both are kept, but on bases that are actually true, because an
executor who tests a cited guard, finds it absent, and concludes the rule was imaginary is a realistic and
expensive failure. Order 01 independently recorded the first of these, so the Set is now consistent on it.

Two smaller things were corrected rather than left to mislead: the reader would have hardcoded the literal
`opencode` while the launch path resolves `options.get("opencode") or "opencode"` (PR-003), and the plan's
timing figures did not reproduce (PR-006: 1.41-1.50s across the same corpus where the plan recorded 4.71s and
1.39s, because the cost is host-start-up-dominated rather than size-dominated). I also found the plan's claim
that the model "sat at offset 263 in all three sessions" to be false in a way that matters mildly: review
measured 262, 365 and 367, the offset moving with the length of the preceding free-form `directory` and
`title` fields. That makes the 4 KiB bound empirical headroom rather than a derived bound, which I recorded
inside OQ-03 as strengthening its own conclusion rather than weakening it.

NOTHING WAS DEFERRED and no question is left open. Suite at review: `3512 passed, 2 skipped, 3 warnings in
174.82s` with 208 deselected, all green, so this plan's executor inherits a clean baseline and any failure in
their run is attributable.
