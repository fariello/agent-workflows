## Workflow history
- 2026-09-22 created (aw backlog): MEASURED 2026-09-22 on the 13 attention.lane-stranded violations that hold 'aw att' at VIEW INVALID after the 36 duplicate-id errors were repaired. EIGHT of the 13 are lanes whose PLAN IS ALREADY 'executed' on main with its work genuinely landed, by a SECOND ATTEMPT that redid the work rather than merging the first lane: 76w6mq, daexj1, i1hlgx, k9awrq, n4xq3l, paw8so, quqyc4, ys1dor. PROVEN rather than assumed for 76w6mq: main's selectors.py contains EVERY function the lane's version defines (comm -23 over the two files' 'def ' sets is empty) and MORE occurrences of the feature symbol (15 vs the lane's 12); the landing commit is 1a0d15b4 'fix(identity): bound identity extraction to the metadata region (76w6mq)' while the lane's own commit is a7452e03, and the two touch the SAME four files with DIFFERENT content (797 insertions on main vs 923 in the lane). So the work landed, by a different commit, from a different attempt. WHY THE PREDICATE CANNOT SEE IT, and this is the actual defect: classify_lane_integration's docstring states its two readings are lane_work_has_landed (ANCESTRY) and lane_work_landed_by_content (present BY PATCH ID, which additionally sees a cherry-pick or rebase). A REDONE lane matches NEITHER: its commits are not ancestors of main and their patch-ids differ because the second attempt wrote different bytes. 'git cherry main <branch>' confirms this mechanically, reporting all commits absent upstream ('+') for every one of the 8 even where the feature is demonstrably present. So the honest state is not STRANDED (work never landed) but SUPERSEDED (this attempt's work was redone and landed another way), and there is no such classification. CONSEQUENCE: these 8 lanes fail the gate permanently. That matters because the surface was built to stop exactly the opposite error - its own header records plan 03ie04 being paid for TWICE because a lane stranded silently - and a gate that reds forever on work that DID land is the failure mode that trains an operator to ignore it, which is how the real next stranding gets missed. A FIX MUST decide how to recognize a superseded lane WITHOUT weakening the stranded check, and the options are not equivalent: (a) consult the PLAN's lifecycle, i.e. a lane whose plan is 'executed' in a terminal directory AND whose id6 appears in a main-side lifecycle commit is superseded rather than stranded, which is cheap and uses records the repo already maintains, but trusts the plan record; (b) compare the lane's touched non-records FILE SET against main and report superseded when main contains every symbol the lane defines, which is evidence-based but needs a language-aware comparison and will not generalize beyond Python; (c) let a human MARK a lane superseded once, durably, with a reason, and have the classifier honor that mark, which is honest and auditable but manual. RECOMMENDATION: (a) plus (c), since (a) covers the measured 8 automatically and (c) is the escape hatch for anything (a) cannot prove. Do NOT fix this by pruning the 8 branches: that makes the symptom disappear while leaving the predicate unable to tell superseded from stranded, so the next redone lane reds the board again. THE REMAINING 5 of the 13 (lc4unl, li44r9, qdd5jq, plus the attempt-suffixed n4xq3l_attempt3 and udgilu_attempt2) are a DIFFERENT case and are NOT covered by this item: lc4unl/li44r9/qdd5jq carry plans still in pending/, so their work genuinely has not landed and the stranded report is CORRECT for them.
