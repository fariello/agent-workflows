## Project conventions discovered (Step 0)

- OWNERSHIP OF THE TRANSITION IS A RUN OPTION. `self_finalize` is frozen into `state["options"]` at queue build (`oc_runipd.py:3047`, `agy_runipd.py:2054`), read back with the default-true idiom `state.get("options", {}).get("self_finalize", True)` (`oc_runipd.py:6100`, `agy_runipd.py:3249`), and both `driver_begin` and `driver_finalize` sit INSIDE `if self_finalize and not is_review:` (`oc_runipd.py:6151`, `agy_runipd.py:3291`). Any prompt statement about who transitions the plan must read that option, not assume it.
- A DRIVER FINALIZE IS NOT GUARANTEED EVEN WHEN OWNED.
