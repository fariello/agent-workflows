## Project conventions discovered (Step 0)

- The vocabularies are single-sourced in `backlog.py`: `PRIORITIES` at `:69` (`frozenset({"high","medium","low"})`), `KINDS` at `:70` (`frozenset({"bug","feature","chore","security","followup"})`). The plan and spec CHECKERS honor that, but the argparse `choices` lists do NOT: they are hand-written literals at `cli.py:1312` (`ipd set`) and `cli.py:4334` (`specs set`). NOTE `backlog new`'s `--work-kind` (`cli.py:4009-4013`) has NO `choices` at all and validates in code (`backlog.py:374-378`), so there are already two registration styles on this field.
- BOTH CLASSIFICATION FIELDS ARE REQUIRED ON A BACKLOG ITEM AND OPTIONAL ON A PLAN OR SPEC. `backlog.validate_item` emits `backlog.priority-invalid` (`:199-205`) and `backlog.kind-invalid` (`:207-213`) when the value is outside the vocabulary, which INCLUDES absent. `tests/test_work_kind.py::BacklogAsymmetryTests` (`:567-594`) exists to assert that asymmetry is deliberate. Measured: a `-` clear on either field yields an invalid item and `aw check backlog` reports `errors 1`.
- `aw backlog set` has TWO dispatch paths, and this is the single most important fact for this plan. The fork tests whether `--status` WAS PASSED (`if getattr(args, "status", None) is None:`, `cli.py:11232`): absent goes to `status_set.run_set_command(..., scoped_type="backlog")` (`:11233-11238`), present sets `args.path` from `args.args[0]` and calls `backlog.run_set` (`:11239-11245`). They do not share a write path. VERIFIED BY EXECUTION at review: the first writes both fields, the second ignores them.
- `status_set.apply_status_change` (`:590`) is record-type-agnostic and writes the optional fields UNCONDITIONALLY on flag presence, outside every status branch, reading them with `getattr(args, "work_kind", None)` (`:767`) and `getattr(args, "priority", None)` (`:754`). That hoisting IS the "persists on a no-op transition" mechanism; there is no same-status early return.
- THE SHARED LINE WRITERS ARE THE ONE WRITE MECHANISM AND THEY DO NOT VALIDATE. `releases.set_work_kind_line` (`:455-475`) and `set_priority_line` (`:434`) are idempotent, full-line-anchored (`_WORK_KIND_LINE_RE` at `:452`), remove the line for `-`/None, and their docstring says the ENUM check "is enforced by `aw check` / validate_spec, not here". `apply_status_change` funnels through them, so any new writer should too.
- `run_set` ALREADY HAS A POST-RENDER LINE-WRITER PRECEDENT: `--blocks-release` is applied AFTER `_render_item` via `releases.set_blocks_release_line` under the comment "Applied after render so `_render_item` stays untouched" (`backlog.py:560-571`). That is the shape to copy, not a parsed-item mutation.
- `backlog._render_item` (`:317-337`) re-emits `item.kind` (`:324`) as parsed, so `run_set` round-trips the field today; a settable override is missing. `run_set` also refuses unless `new_status in STATUSES` (`:479-483`), so this spelling cannot express a flags-only call.
- `tests/test_work_kind.py` contains a deliberate `BacklogAsymmetryTests` class (`:567-594`) asserting the backlog side REQUIRES its field and reports through its own rule. Read it before touching backlog validation, so this plan's change is recognized as adding a setter rather than relaxing a requirement.
- `aw backlog new` already accepts `--work-kind` (`cli.py:3921-3926`) with a retained `--kind` alias (`:3927-3932`), applied at `backlog.py:363-365` with the preferred spelling winning. Match that spelling precedent; do NOT add a `--kind` alias to `set`, since nothing has ever shipped one there and a new alias is a new deprecation obligation.
