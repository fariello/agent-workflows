## Goal

Make it impossible for a plan to reach `executed` with an empty declared-scope delta WITHOUT that fact being written into its permanent record. Today such a finalize is reconciled clean by construction, records nothing a later auditor can read. After this plan the executed plan and its lifecycle commit both state that no declared path was modified, so the silent case has a permanent, auditable surface instead of none. A RUN-LEVEL signal (the runner noticing the shape in the same run) is NOT delivered here: the zero-work predicate cannot fire on a self-finalized turn by construction (F-13), and the correct shape for that signal is deferred to `gmbdxe`. The finalize VERDICT is deliberately unchanged: the same finalizes succeed, no exit code moves, and no legitimate execution is refused.
