## Goal

When a lane's suite goes red on a corpus trap, make the failure report say so, on the failing test, naming the live paths it read. That converts a recurring cost ("review prose re-establishing that the red is nobody's fault", which `29wvmj` calls a CORPUS TRAP) into a line the agent reads for free.

Equally: record, with the numbers, why the guard the item sketched must NOT be built as sketched. This plan is deliberately a diagnostic and not a gate. A gate here would either deselect 38 passing tests or institutionalize an allowlist the size of the population it is meant to police.

## The detector's four bounds

E-06 transcribes these into the plugin docstring. They are stated here, once, so the docstring is a
transcription rather than a fresh judgement an executing agent has to make under time pressure.

- BOUND NOT-A-GATE. It fails nothing and deselects nothing. A corpus trap still reds the suite exactly
  as it does today; the only claim is that the red now NAMES its cause. Anyone reading this as a
  protection against the 2026-09-19 incident recurring has misread it: the incident would recur, with
  a better error message.
- BOUND SUBPROCESS-BLIND. An in-process audit hook cannot observe a read performed by a spawned
  `python3 -m agent_workflows`, and this suite spawns the CLI often through `tests/support.run_cli`.
  A corpus trap reached only that way therefore gets no note at all.
- BOUND READING-IS-NOT-A-DEFECT. This is why the note is worded as information and never as an
  accusation. Many of the 38 touch the corpus incidentally (an `aw status` smoke test asserting only
  its output envelope) or read ONE named artifact (`tests/test_runner_shared.py`'s check on spec
  `25kzda`). Neither shape can be reddened by a third party's new artifact. The shape that CAN is the
  subset asserting over a SET enumerated at runtime, and separating those two mechanically would mean
  judging the assertion rather than the read, which is precisely what no detector here attempts.
- BOUND NO-CAUSATION. A test may read the corpus and fail for a wholly unrelated reason. The note
  reports that a live read happened during a failing test; the reader still judges whether it mattered.
