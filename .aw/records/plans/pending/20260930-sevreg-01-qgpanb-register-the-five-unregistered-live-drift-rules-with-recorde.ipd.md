## Project conventions discovered (Step 0)

- SEVERITY HAS ONE AUTHORITY AND TWO READ PATHS, and this plan turns on the difference. `check_engine.RULE_REGISTRY` read through `check_engine.rule_spec` is the registry; `check_engine.enrich_drift` stamps it onto a `Drift` as `drift.severity or spec.severity`, so an emitter's own stamp WINS over the registry. `artifact_core.drift_exit_code` then reads the stamped field. A registered rule whose emitter never enriches is therefore invisible to the gate, which is the gap E-04 closes.
- A `RULE_REGISTRY` ENTRY CARRIES A PROSE JUSTIFICATION FOR ITS SEVERITY, and several in-tree entries are the precedent this plan follows. `check.spec-criteria-uncovered` records "Severity is load-bearing: artifact_core.drift_exit_code exempts ONLY `info`" beside its `info`/`""` pair; `check.lifecycle-placement-conflict` records why `error` rather than `warning` and why its invariant is `""`; `check.stale-index-missing` records why an absent manifest is informational.
- `info` IS THE ONLY ADVISORY TIER. `artifact_core.drift_exit_code`'s one-line body exempts exactly `info`, so `warning` fails a findings gate identically to `error`. A rule intended to report without failing must be `info`; there is no middle option, whatever the word suggests.
- A RULE ID MAY NOT CONTAIN `graduation` OR `duplicate`. `tests/test_check_engine_spec_criteria.py::test_rule_id_contains_neither_graduation_nor_duplicate` asserts that no key in `RULE_REGISTRY` contains either substring. All five ids this plan registers are already clear of both, so the guard is satisfied without renaming anything; it is recorded so an executor does not have to discover the constraint.
- MISSING AND STALE ARE DISTINCT RULES, and the twin comments in `plans_index.check_drift` and `research_index.check_drift` record why: an absent manifest is the normal state of a fresh clone (hence `info`), while a present-but-not-byte-equal manifest is real drift. Those comments are also where the enrich-at-the-emitter convention E-04 extends is already written down.
- Cite code by SYMBOL (`module.function`) or by a quoted content string, with a line number only appended to one of those and never alone: an offset expires before this plan executes (spec `ipd-structure-and-linting` Section 10.2; advisory `IPD-C801`).
