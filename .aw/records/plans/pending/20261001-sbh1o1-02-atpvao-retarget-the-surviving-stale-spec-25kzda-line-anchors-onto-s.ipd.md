## Project conventions discovered (Step 0)

- Cite code by SYMBOL (`module.function`) or by a quoted content string, with a line number only appended to one of those and never alone: an offset expires before this plan executes (spec `ipd-structure-and-linting` Section 10.2; advisory `IPD-C801`). THIS PLAN IS THE CONVENTION APPLIED TO ITSELF, and it names every one of its 21 target sites by symbol for that reason.
- SPEC `25kzda` DECLARES ITS OWN PREAMBLE UNSTABLE and instructs re-measurement: "EVERY DATED PARAGRAPH BELOW IS A POINT-IN-TIME SNAPSHOT, NOT A STANDING CLAIM, AND YOU MUST RE-MEASURE BEFORE RELYING ON ANY OF IT", adding the honest limit that "NOTHING ENFORCES THIS ... no test and no `aw check` rule reads these paragraphs". E-05 is what finally puts a rule behind the anchor half of that instruction.
- `RULE_REGISTRY` ENTRIES CARRY A PROSE JUSTIFICATION FOR THEIR SEVERITY, and `check.spec-criteria-uncovered` and `check.scope-path-target-stale` are the in-tree `info`/`""` precedents that `mt54wr` E-05 followed. E-05 here follows the same convention in the opposite direction, replacing the justification rather than deleting it.
- `artifact_core.drift_exit_code` EXEMPTS ONLY `info`, so there is no "warn but pass" tier: promoting past `info` makes the check fail. That single fact is why this sweep had to precede the promotion and is why E-05 refuses to promote on a nonzero census.
- A TERMINAL RECORD'S CITATION IS NOT A DEFECT TO FIX. The execution contract forbids changing what a plan in `plans/executed/` records, so stale anchors inside terminal plans and done backlog items are out of scope by rule, not by preference.
- A CROSS-PLAN TRIPWIRE IS WORSE THAN NO PIN (`6uhtko` PR/F-14, reused by `mt54wr` E-06): pinning a live tree census "fires on a legitimate change rather than on a regression". E-06 obeys this and deliberately declines to assert the real census is zero.

### OQ-01: Should the retargeted citations name a section NUMBER or a section TITLE, given that a renumbered section rots a number just as a line anchor rots an offset?

- Blocking: no
- Status: resolved
- Owner: atpvao
- Resolution or deferral rationale: RESOLVED AT AUTHORING IN FAVOUR OF SECTION NUMBERS (`Section 2.1`), on two grounds.
