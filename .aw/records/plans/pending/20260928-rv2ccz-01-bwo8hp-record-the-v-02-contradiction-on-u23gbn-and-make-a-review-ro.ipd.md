## Project conventions discovered (Step 0)

- AN EXECUTED PLAN IS APPEND-ONLY, NOT IMMUTABLE, and that distinction is the whole mechanism of E-01. `AGENTS.md` forbids changing what such a plan RECORDS and in the same breath permits appending a dated history line pointing at later work. So "the contradiction stays readable in an executed plan" is answerable without rewriting anything, and rewriting V-02's text would in any case destroy the audit trail of a review round that erred.
- PLAN HISTORY IN THIS REPOSITORY IS NEWEST-FIRST, and readers depend on it: `.aw/records/plans/README.md` states a new record "is inserted directly under the `## Workflow history` heading, so the FIRST record is the most recent", and names `plan_readiness.extract_newest_history_entry` as the reader, recording that a reader which "sliced to end of file and took the last bullet is the bug that fix replaced". Hence E-01's placement constraint.
- A HISTORY LINE'S LEADING TOKEN DISTINGUISHES A TRANSITION FROM A NOTE. `ipd_lifecycle._PLAN_STATUS_VOCAB` holds the ten tokens that are a STATUS transition, and `ipd_lifecycle._plan_status_events` skips any line whose token is not one of them, documenting that "annotations never masquerade as transitions".
