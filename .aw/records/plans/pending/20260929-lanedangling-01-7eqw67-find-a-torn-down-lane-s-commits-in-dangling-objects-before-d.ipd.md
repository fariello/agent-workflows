## Goal

Make this repository stop concluding "the work is gone" from a question that was only ever asked about refs, in the two places that draw that conclusion: the plan-review evidence convention, which measurably retired a recoverable replay source and weakened a validation item to a synthetic fallback, and the `REINTEGRATE_LANE_ABSENT` refusal, which tells an operator "there is nothing to integrate" on the same refs-only evidence.

WHAT THIS GOAL DOES NOT CLAIM, stated because the adjacent fix is easy to conflate with it. It does NOT make dangling objects durable, and no artifact this plan writes may imply that: they are local to one checkout, are not cloned, and gc prunes them on git's own schedule. A dangling commit is a legitimate replay source WHEN IT RESOLVES and is never an archive. It also does NOT keep newly abandoned commits reachable - that is plan `c8ioct`'s subject via `refs/aw/abandoned/coordinator/*`, and the two are complements: `c8ioct` prevents the condition going forward, this plan makes the existing population findable. And it does NOT change what `LANE_ABSENT` MEANS; ABSENT is a correct statement about refs, and only the inference drawn from it changes.

The honest one-line summary is "before declaring lane evidence unrecoverable, look in the objects, and say what you actually measured".
