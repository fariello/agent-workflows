- A USER-PERCEPTIBLE DELAY IS A DEFECT in this repository (`59t9x5`, reclassified `bug` by the maintainer for costing ~128ms of a ~530ms command), which bounds how expensive this guard may be. VERIFIED at review: `59t9x5` is `open`, `Work-Kind: bug`, `Blocks-Release: next`, with that exact summary. - `backlog.run_new` ALREADY WALKS AND FULLY READS EVERY BACKLOG ITEM, to build the id6 collision set (`_iter_items` then `parse_item(...).id` into `existing_ids`, consumed by `core.mint_id6`). So the guard's corpus read need not be a new walk, and adding a second one would be the `59t9x5` double-read pattern in miniature.

All three verified at review; note `caf5ed`'s specific warning is about arrangements that "pass VACUOUSLY", which is the failure a fixture-based test is most prone to, hence E-03's fail-first requirement.
- A RECORDS POPULATION MOVES UNDER A PLAN. This plan's own motivating corpus went from 23 `open` to 23 `graduated` between authoring and review (F-7), which is why E-01 counts per status and why E-03 must not read the live tree.

## Open questions

### OQ-01: Distinctive-token overlap, prose similarity, or both?

- Blocking: no
- Status: resolved
- Owner: this plan's executor
- Carrier-Declined: resolved at review from measured evidence; no obligation survives for a future artifact to carry.
- Resolution or deferral rationale: RESOLVED AT REVIEW, AND THE DECIDING NUMBER WAS MEASURED RATHER THAN PREDICTED. The original rationale said token overlap "carries almost no false-positive risk" and set the bar "if token overlap catches 23 of 23 with no unrelated matches, do not build similarity scoring". Half of that is confirmed and half is falsified: `test_turn_bounds` catches 23 of 23 (perfect recall) AND matches 12 unrelated items (66 percent precision), so the literal bar is unreachable. THE ANSWER IS TOKEN OVERLAP PLUS A CHEAP DISCRIMINATOR, NOT PROSE SIMILARITY. Token overlap stays the primary signal because its recall is perfect, it is explainable to the filer in one line, and it costs a corpus read that `run_new` already performs. Its precision problem is addressable without fuzzy matching, by requiring CO-OCCURRENCE of two distinctive tokens (node id AND variable name), by weighting a token by its rarity across the corpus, or by matching the `- Summary:` field rather than the whole body; E-01 measures whichever is chosen and reports its precision. PROSE SIMILARITY REMAINS REJECTED for this plan: it is where the remaining false positives live, the measured recall gap it would close is ZERO (token overlap already catches every member of the corpus), and the advisory's usefulness is bounded by precision rather than recall. That reasoning is the inverse of the original framing and is stated so a reader does not think the conclusion was reached by the same route.

### OQ-02: Should the 1.7-second `mint_id6` cost be filed as its own defect?

- Blocking: no
- Status: open
- Owner: maintainer
- Carrier-Declined: raised rather than performed, and deliberately given no carrier id6 because no existing artifact describes it; filing one is the remedy and is a filing decision, not a reviewer's.
- Resolution or deferral rationale: NOT blocking on this plan, whose guard is unaffected either way, but recorded because F-8 measured it and losing it would be the exact "obligation vanishes at retirement" failure the carrier rule exists to prevent. THE MEASUREMENT: `aw backlog new` (dry run) costs about 1.9 to 2.7 seconds end to end; `artifact_core.mint_id6` is about 1.7 to 1.9 seconds of that, spent in `artifact_adopt.repository_id6s` scanning the whole repository for 1473 ids; the entire backlog corpus walk plus parse is about 22 to 50ms. RECOMMENDATION: file it `--work-kind bug --blocks-release next`, because the repository's own `59t9x5` ruling makes a user-perceptible delay on a waited-on command a bug rather than a chore, and this is roughly 90 percent of a command's wall time rather than the 24 percent that ruling was applied to. THE HONEST CAVEAT, so the filing is not overstated: I did not investigate whether the scan is REDUNDANT (as `59t9x5`'s double read was) or merely expensive-but-necessary for correct global collision avoidance; `mint_id6`'s docstring argues the repository-wide set is deliberate and "strictly stronger" than the per-tree check. So the defect to file is the COST, with the open question being whether it can be cached or narrowed without weakening the collision guarantee. A fix that reintroduces id6 collisions would be far worse than the delay.
