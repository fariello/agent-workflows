## Goal

Make "is this failure retryable?" answerable from ONE contract that a test can check against the spec, so the question stops being re-derived by judgement at each consumer. After this plan spec `25kzda` §5.5 states, in a table, which driver disposition each of its retry classes maps onto; `TURN_RETRYABLE_DISPOSITIONS` is DERIVED from `TURN_RETRY_CLASSIFICATION` rather than maintained beside it, so the two cannot disagree again; the one row on which they currently DO disagree is resolved deliberately and recorded; every disposition in the drivers' closed vocabulary has a stated verdict; and the coverage invariant the shipped comment claims is actually enforced by a test that exists.

THE GOAL IS ONE CHECKABLE MAPPING, NOT A REWRITE OF RETRY POLICY. No disposition becomes retryable that is not retryable today except `failed`, whose resolution is the whole point of the exercise and is decided from evidence in E-02 rather than by preference. The never-retryable classes stay never-retryable, both consumers keep failing closed, and `finalize_refusal_is_retryable`'s prose arms are untouched so pending plan `qo9khm` can re-key them onto lint codes without a conflict.
