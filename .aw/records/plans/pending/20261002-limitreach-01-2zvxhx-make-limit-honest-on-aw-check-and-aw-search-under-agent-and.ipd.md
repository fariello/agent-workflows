- Concern: `aw check <type> --agent --limit N` and `aw search <type> <pattern> --agent --limit N` accept a documented token-control flag and discard it. `docs/cli-output-contract.md` Section 6 lists `--limit <N>` as one of three escape hatches, promising it "Bounds stream item emission to at most `N` items and includes total counts, omitted counts, and a continuation command in the terminating `summary` record", with no stated per-verb restriction. ### OQ-01: Should `check` and `search` honor `--limit` by emitting an item stream, by refusing the flag, or by bounding the payload inside their existing record

- Blocking: no
- Status: resolved
- Owner: plan author
- Resolution or deferral rationale: RESOLVED FROM REPOSITORY EVIDENCE AT AUTHORING: direction (c), bound the payload in the existing `result` record and report `total`/`emitted`/`omitted` plus `next`, on BOTH verbs. ### OQ-02: Should a non-positive `--limit` be refused, clamped, or treated as unset

- Blocking: no
- Status: resolved
- Owner: plan author
- Resolution or deferral rationale: REFUSED AT EXIT 2 WITH A `cannot-run` RECORD on both verbs, resolved at authoring so no open obligation leaves this plan.
