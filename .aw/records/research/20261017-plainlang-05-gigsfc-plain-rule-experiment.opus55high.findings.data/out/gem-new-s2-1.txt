The second task identifier (`x9z0ve`) required a retry and was still completing verification under the provisional `fail-gate` status when the screenshot was taken at 22:40:24. Both items completed with status `executed` and verification status `verified`.

**What fixes it:** Update the progress tally function (`render_stream.compute_queue_tally`) to treat provisional holding statuses like `fail-gate` as in-progress rather than failed until a task reaches a terminal state.
