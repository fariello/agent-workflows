## 2. Regulatory Context and Grandfathering Mechanism

### Why `evaluate_blocking_close` Evaluates These as Illegitimate
Under `check_engine.evaluate_blocking_close`, closing a release-gated backlog item `done` is legitimate if and only if one of three paths is satisfied:
1. **HANDOFF**: Every From-Backlog plan or spec carrying the same `Blocks-Release` gate is `executed` / `implemented`.
2. **SATISFIED**: Resolvable evidence artifact citation (via `--evidence` or `- Close-Evidence:`).
3. **DE-GATED**: The item no longer carries `Blocks-Release`.

The 53 historical items audited here satisfy none of these three criteria on disk, so `evaluate_blocking_close` yields `legitimate=False, severity="error"`.

### Why `aw check release-gates` Reports 0 Errors on a Clean Tree
Plan `b24o3q` (executed 2026-10-01, commit `d24e81a83`) introduced an at-rest arm to `check_release_gate_consistency` (Rule 1). However, that arm grandfathers items against the stamped `release_gate_at_rest` cutover date (`2026-10-01` in `.aw/config/project.json`). Because the newest of the 53 items closed on `2026-09-26`, all 53 items predate the cutover date and are grandfathered from the exit-blocking sweep.

### Strict Read-Only Policy: No Mutation of Closed Records
`AGENTS.md` explicitly establishes that release gates govern **live** items only. Writing a gate onto, clearing a gate from, or re-opening an already-done backlog item "would assert a history that did not happen". Consequently, this audit is strictly diagnostic: **no backlog record has been or will be mutated**.

## 3. Structural Limits on the Evidence

Two critical structural facts constrain the interpretation of these findings:

1. **`--evidence` was NOT persisted on disk prior to plan `f7igdu` (commit `47f7d9727`, 2026-10-01)**:
   Historically, `backlog.run_set` read `--evidence` and passed it to the validation predicate, but never serialized `- Close-Evidence:` onto the backlog item markdown. Therefore, an item legitimately closed with valid evidence prior to 2026-10-01 is byte-indistinguishable on disk from an item closed without evidence. All 53 items in this audit closed on or before 2026-09-26. Thus, **every finding is a candidate and the 53 figure is an upper bound on genuine gate drops, never a count of proven violations**.
2. **Retrospective Evaluation**:
   The audit evaluates the tree at current execution HEAD. If a carrier was pending when an item was closed but has since executed, the predicate evaluates it as legitimate today. Conversely, carriers superseded or moved after the close reflect the current disk state, not historical close-time state.
